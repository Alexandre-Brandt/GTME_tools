### What is the Email Finder subscription?

▼

You can use the Dropcontact Email Finder in different ways:

* **File drag-and-drop**: Drop off your database file, Dropcontact will enrich it, and you simply download your new enriched file
* **Unique research**: You can search business email addresses one by one
* **API's Dropcontact:** You can use Dropcontact thanks to its API or into a lot of partners that integrate Dropcontact

### How does the Email Finder subscription work?

▼

You can choose the quota of emails found (monthly or annually) that you want, renewed upon expiration.  
If you opt for the 500 emails/month subscription, you will get 500 emails found.  
  
In case an email is not found by Dropcontact, it will be re-credited to you.  
🔥 With the "Credit Carryover" option, you accumulate and keep the emails you haven't found from one month to the next. The only condition is to have an active paid subscription.

‍[👉🏼 **More information about the Email Finder subscription**](https://support.dropcontact.com/article/212-how-does-the-subscription-work)

### Once I've subscribed, can I modify it?

▼

Dropcontact operates as a monthly or annual subscription that is automatically renewable.  
  
Each month, you have a quota of email addresses, renewed on each due date.The subscription is flexible and adjustable according to your needs.  
  
You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time by simply clicking the upgrade button in the Dropcontact application.

### What if I only need Dropcontact for a single file enrichment?

▼

😊 No problem! Dropcontact works on a monthly subscription with no commitment required. You can subscribe to any plan, process your file, and cancel right after.When you cancel, it takes effect at your next billing date, giving you a full month to use your credits.  
  
So you can subscribe, enrich your database, and cancel immediately, you'll still have access until the end of your billing period.  
  
And if you still have unused credits after processing your file, you can downgrade to a lower plan using our Credit Rollover feature, which lets you keep your credits for future use at a reduced cost.

‍[👉🏼  **Learn more about vabout subscription flexibility**](https://support.dropcontact.com/category/279-my-account)

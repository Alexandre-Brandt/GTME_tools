One API key, the whole data layer: companies, people, verified emails, phone numbers and job postings. Plans differ by speed, fair use policy and support, never by data.

For your first workflows in production

## Frequently asked questions

Start is $99 a month for 10 requests per second per endpoint, under a fair use policy of 200,000 records a month. Build Your Infra starts at $999 a month and is fully custom, scoped on a call. Prices are in US dollars, billed monthly, and every account begins with 1,000 free records, no card.

The number of records your plan covers each month: 200,000 on Start, 5,000,000 on Scale, a custom volume on Build Your Infra. It resets at the start of each billing cycle, unused records do not carry over, and it is written into section 9 of the Terms rather than decided account by account.

One record is one item the API returns to you: one company, one person, one email address, one phone number or one job posting. A call that finds nothing counts nothing. A people search that returns 25 profiles counts 25 records; an email lookup that returns one verified address counts one. Records are the unit of the fair use policy, and the unit the Terms call a Record.

How fast a job finishes. The rate applies per endpoint: each endpoint has its own budget, so email lookups and company searches run side by side without slowing each other. A live lookup feels instant on both; the difference shows on batch runs across a whole market.

It depends on how the API bills. Many B2B data APIs charge per credit, so the invoice grows with every lookup and a large enrichment job has a price before it runs. BlitzAPI bills a flat monthly plan instead: the price includes a request rate and a fair use policy counted in records, with every endpoint open. The plans and their prices are on this page.

Verified means tested, not guessed. Each email passes three layers of verification, including on domains that accept every address, which most tools mark as a maybe. We never guess from a name pattern: an address we cannot prove comes back Not Found.

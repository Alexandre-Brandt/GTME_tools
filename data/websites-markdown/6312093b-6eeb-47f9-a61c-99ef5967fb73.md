GeneralFeatureSetup & Sending

**What is Mission Inbox?**

Mission Inbox provides dedicated email infrastructure for cold email and sales outreach, helping sales teams and agencies run outbound campaigns with reliable mailboxes and dedicated IPs.

**Does Mission Inbox replace Google Workspace or Microsoft 365?**

Yes.

**Is Mission Inbox a sequencer?**

No. Mission Inbox does not build campaigns, manage contacts or write sequences. It works underneath your CRM, sequencer, product or existing tools.

**Who is Mission Inbox built for?**

Mission Inbox is built for sales teams and agencies running cold email and sales outreach at scale.

**What types of email can I send?**

You can send cold email and sales outreach through Mission Inbox mailboxes connected to your sequencer. Mission Inbox does not offer transactional, product-notification or promotional-email sending.

**What are Isolated Cubes?**

Isolated Cubes are separate sending environments for your cold outreach. Keep clients, brands, campaigns and domains isolated so one campaign does not put another’s sending reputation at risk.

What is MI Shield™?

MI Shield checks content, headers, DNS and spam signals before emails are sent. It helps catch risky emails before they damage your domain or reputation.

**Do I get dedicated IPs?**

Yes. Mission Inbox uses dedicated IPs and isolated infrastructure instead of placing your sending inside crowded shared pools.

**Can I see what happens after emails are sent?**

Yes. You can view analytics, logs, delivery activity, bounces, sending health and performance from inside the platform.

**What happens if one route has an issue?**

Mission Inbox is built with redundancy across three continents. If one route has a problem, email can continue moving through another.

**How fast can I get set up?**

Mission Inbox is built for setup in 10 minutes. You can also get help from the team if you want support getting everything connected.

**Can I keep using my existing tools?**

Yes. Mission Inbox can work with your current mailbox, CRM, sequencer, product or API workflow.

**Where do replies go?**

Replies go back to the mailbox your team already uses, so conversations stay inside your normal workflow.

**Can I test Mission Inbox before connecting my own setup?**

Yes. Demo Mode lets you explore the full platform with preloaded domains, mailboxes, messages, analytics and logs.

**When can I send live email?**

You can preview the platform in Demo Mode first. To connect your own setup and send live email, choose a paid plan and start sending.

## Build a professional website from canvas to publishing

1. 1. 1. Start with a site idea
   2. Begin with a blank canvas, prompt, template, or Figma design, then shape the first version around your brand, pages, and goals. Framer gives you a flexible starting point that can become a polished website without leaving the visual canvas.
2. 1. 2. Design every page visually
   2. Refine layout, copy, images, responsive breakpoints, and interactions directly on the canvas. You can keep iterating on the site visually without setting up or managing a separate codebase.
3. 1. 3. Add content and SEO
   2. Connect CMS content, set metadata, tune page structure, and prepare every page for search before launch. Framer keeps content, SEO, performance, and design controls together in one workflow.
4. 1. 4. Publish and keep improving
   2. Launch with hosting, staging, custom domains, redirects, and analytics, then keep updating the site visually over time. Your team can improve pages after launch without rebuilding the project.

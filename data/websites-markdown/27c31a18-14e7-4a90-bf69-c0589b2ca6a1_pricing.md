Pricing FAQ

## RB2B pricing questions

[See all RB2B FAQs >>](/faq)![](/images/detective-mascot.svg)

### How much does RB2B cost?

RB2B has a free plan and paid plans from $79/mo. Free is $0 (Company-Level ID, 150 resolutions/mo). Starter is $79/mo (Person-Level ID, LinkedIn URLs to Slack or Teams, 300 resolutions/mo). Pro starts at $149/mo (business emails and all integrations, from 600 resolutions/mo). Pro+ starts at $199/mo (Premium Resolution with higher match rates, from 600 resolutions/mo).

### Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every account gets a 7-day Pro+ trial (or 1,000 credits, whichever comes first), and no credit card is required. After the trial you can stay on the Free plan (company-level ID, 150 resolutions/mo) or upgrade.

### What is a resolution?

A resolution is one identified website visitor that RB2B delivers to you. Each plan includes a number of monthly resolutions, and higher Pro and Pro+ tiers add more (up to 2,500 on Pro and up to 2,500,000 on Pro+).

### What happens if I go over my monthly resolutions?

Additional resolutions are billed as overage: 45¢ per resolution on Starter and 25¢ per resolution on Pro and Pro+.

### What's the difference between Pro and Pro+?

Pro+ uses Premium Resolution. It adds the Identity Graph Waterfall and raises coverage from 15-20% to 35-45% for both contact-level (US) and company-level (global) ID. Both plans include business emails, Hot Pages, Hot Leads, exclusions and all integrations.

### Which integrations are included in each plan?

Slack and Teams are on every plan. Pro and Pro+ add Clay, HubSpot, Salesforce, Zapier, Webhook, Apollo, CSV export and over a dozen other products. [See all integrations](/integrations).

### Can I use RB2B on more than one website?

Yes. Pro and Pro+ customers can add domains for $99/mo (up to 5). Agencies managing client sites can use the [agency program](/agencies).

### Does RB2B pricing include visitors outside the US?

Company-Level ID is global on every plan. Contact-level (person) ID is US only. [Read more in the FAQ](/faq).

### What support is included?

Every plan includes the self-serve knowledge base plus AI chat and AI email support, with human escalation on every plan.

### I need more than 12,500 resolutions a month. How do I get pricing?

Pro+ is self-serve all the way up to 2,500,000 resolutions a month, so you can pick a higher tier on the pricing page without talking to sales. [See pricing](/pricing).

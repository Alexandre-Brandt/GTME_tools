# Competitor price scraping

Price scraping is the automated collection of competitor prices from online stores and marketplaces: selling price, list price, promotion, shipping and stock for every product you track. Zenrows fetches those pages from protected retail sites, returns the fields as JSON, and refreshes your SKU list on a schedule with Batch.

## What price monitoring teams collect

Start from your products and the competitor URLs that sell them.

Then choose how often to read them and which fields to keep.

| Job | Data you collect | Typical sources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Competitor price tracking | Selling and list price per SKU | Amazon, Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Home Depot |
| Promotion and markdown tracking | Sale badges, coupons, bundles, dates | Product and deal pages, Amazon Today's Deals |
| Delivered price | Shipping fee, free-shipping threshold, delivery date | Product and cart pages |
| Marketplace offers | Lowest offer, seller count, featured seller | Amazon, Walmart Marketplace, eBay, Mercado Livre |
| Regional and country pricing | Local price and currency | MediaMarkt, Carrefour, Allegro, Flipkart, Amazon country sites |
| Grocery price tracking | Shelf and unit price per store or postcode | Tesco, Sainsbury's, Carrefour, Woolworths, Instacart |
| Price history | Every price, timestamped | Your past runs |

## Why price scraping breaks

Retailers protect prices hardest and change them most.

Failures are quiet: a 200 with the price missing, stale or from the wrong market.

A CAPTCHA inside a 200, or 403s after a few hundred requests

Zenrows' stealth gets through AWS WAF, PerimeterX (HUMAN) and Akamai

Empty or placeholder price in the raw HTML

Adaptive Stealth Mode renders the page only when the price needs it

Price and stock load from a JSON endpoint

Zenrows captures the XHR calls; read the price from the response

The US price or currency when you track the UK store

Geotargeting requests from the country you price

A price from another store than your customer's

Set the store per task, by cookie or Browser session

The struck-through price instead of the selling one

One CSS selector per field keeps `price` and `list_price` apart

## Price scraping and monitoring FAQ

The automated collection of prices from websites. Retail teams read competitor product pages on a schedule and keep price, promotion, shipping and stock per product, building a price history that feeds pricing decisions.

A tool gives dashboards and matching for the retailers it supports. An API gives raw prices from any retailer, at your cadence, in your warehouse. Teams with a data engineer build on an API; teams that want a screen buy a tool.

As often as prices move: daily for most categories, every few hours for electronics, marketplaces and grocery promotions. Before Black Friday, raise it for top sellers only.

By GTIN, UPC, EAN or part number where the page shows one; otherwise by brand and model, checked by hand for top sellers. See [product matching](/use-cases/product-matching).

The analysis on top of scraped prices: your price index per competitor, where you are cheapest, promotion depth, and how fast rivals follow you. Scraping supplies the observations; matching and history make it intelligence.

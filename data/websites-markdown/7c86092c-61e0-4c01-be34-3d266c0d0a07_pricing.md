Every plan includes the Kiflo MCP server, so your team can work with Kiflo from Claude or ChatGPT: ask questions on live data, register leads and deals, update records, create tasks and pull reports. No extra fee.

Enterprise PRMs are built for large channel organizations, with the price, complexity, and multi-month rollouts that come with that. Kiflo is built for SaaS teams building or scaling partner-led revenue: it's live in under a week, starts at $4,788 per year, and covers the work your team does every week, which you can run from Claude or ChatGPT. If you run a large enterprise channel, those suites fit. If you want partners driving revenue without a six-month implementation, that is Kiflo.

A spreadsheet or CRM can log partners, but it can't run the program: give partners a way to register leads, apply registration rules, or calculate commissions from tracked revenue. Past a handful of partners, the manual work and the missed revenue cost more than Kiflo does. And because Kiflo connects to HubSpot and Salesforce, partner activity lives alongside your pipeline instead of in a separate sheet.

You can build workflows on top of Kiflo in minutes. Building the system underneath is another job: partner access and permissions, registration rules, commission history finance can audit, CRM sync, security, and someone to maintain it all. That's what Kiflo does.

No. Kiflo is useful for teams launching their ecosystem and for teams scaling an existing one. If you are still building, Kiflo helps you put the foundation in place. If you already have partners, Kiflo helps you activate them around target accounts and revenue.

Fast. Kiflo is live in under a week. You connect your CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce) and bring your partners. Partners can register deals right away, and your team can connect Claude or ChatGPT on day one. Then layer in co-sell, tracking, and commissions as the program grows.

Kiflo pricing is based on how many active partners you have. An active partner is a partner that has at least one user that has access to the Partner Portal. So if they have multiple users, they are only counted as one active partner. Partners saved in Kiflo are not counted as active partners until you give them access to the Partner Portal.

Each of your partners can have an unlimited number of users. There’s no cap, so they’re free to have as many team members as they need!

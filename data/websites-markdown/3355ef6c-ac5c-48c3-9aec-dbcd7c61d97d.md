## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### What is Modjo?

Modjo is the AI-powered revenue intelligence platform that turns every conversation into actionable data. Our AI agents transcribe your calls, update your CRM, and spot key business signals throughout the entire sales cycle.

### How much does Modjo cost?

Modjo's pricing is based on the number of recording licenses, the users who record and analyse their calls. Listener licenses, for managers and leadership, are unlimited and included at no extra cost. Every subscription includes full access to all Modjo features, a personalised onboarding, and a dedicated Account Manager.

### Where is Modjo data hosted?

Modjo data is hosted in France, on AWS (Amazon Web Services) servers located in the Paris region. Modjo is SOC 2 Type II certified and fully GDPR compliant. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

### How do Modjo integrations work?

Modjo connects to your entire sales stack so your teams never have to switch tools. [**Modjo is natively accessible within your CRM**](https://www.modjo.ai/fr/book-a-demo/modjo-salesforce) via a Chrome extension, bringing the power of our AI Agents directly where your reps already work. Modjo also offers an open API and an MCP server to embed all conversation data into your own workflows and internal tools.

### How long does it take to deploy Modjo?

Modjo deploys quickly for every user, connecting tools to the AI agents takes just 5 to 10 minutes. Prior to that, a dedicated Account Manager runs a structured 30-day onboarding: technical setup, manager and rep training, and activation of your identified use cases.

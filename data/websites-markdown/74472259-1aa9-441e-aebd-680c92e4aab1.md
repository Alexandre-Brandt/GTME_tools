## FAQ

Quick answers to the most common questions about Scripe.

Scripe is the social media content system for B2B teams: a social media management platform built around LinkedIn. It runs your content as one system, from proven ideas and drafts to approvals, publishing and analytics, across your company page, executives and employees, so a whole team posts on strategy and social contributes to pipeline.

Social media management software plans, schedules and publishes posts. A social media content system also decides what to post and learns from what worked: your positioning sets the topics, ideas come from posts proven in your market, drafts fit each person's profile, and what worked shapes next month. Scripe is a social media content system for B2B teams.

Social media management software helps a team plan, create, schedule, publish and analyze content across their social accounts from one place, instead of juggling separate tools. Scripe focuses this on B2B and LinkedIn, across your company page and your people, from proven ideas through to analytics.

Social media content management comes down to content strategy and ideas, a content calendar, scheduling and publishing, collaboration and approval workflows, analytics and reporting, and a shared content library. Scripe adds outlier-proven ideas, drafts shaped by each person's background and point of view, employee advocacy and link tracking on top.

One system replaces a stack of disconnected tools, so you stop losing time stitching them together. You get a whole company posting proven, on-strategy content, each person with their own point of view, approvals without shared logins, and analytics that tie social to pipeline. And the ideas get better the more you post.

General suites help one brand post across many networks. Scripe is built for B2B teams on LinkedIn: it activates your executives and employees, not just the company page, grounds content in your own knowledge, starts from ideas proven against their author's baseline, and shows what your posts deliver.

Generic AI writes a post when you prompt it. Scripe starts from ideas proven in your market, grounds each draft in your positioning and knowledge, and runs the approvals, publishing and analytics around it: the system, not a single text box. You can also connect Claude and ChatGPT to Scripe.

Scripe connects through LinkedIn's official interface via OAuth, without your colleagues' login details. By default every post starts as a draft, and only what you approve goes live. Likes, comments and reposts from colleagues go out from their own profiles, and each person decides per type: automatic, after their OK, or never. Content is stored in Frankfurt, with a DPA included.

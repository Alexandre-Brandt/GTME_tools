### How many credits would you like to buy?

Search credits are used to find emails through the [Domain Search](/domain-search) or [Email Finder](/email-finder), and Verification credits are used to verify emails with the [Email Verifier](/email-verifier). More details can be found [here](https://help.hunter.io/en/articles/1911617-what-is-a-credit-and-how-is-it-counted).

Each repeated search or verification is counted once per calendar month.

## Common questions about pricing

If you can't find the answer to your question here, visit the dedicated section in our Help Center.

[Visit the Help Center](https://help.hunter.io/en/collections/99840-billing-subscriptions)

### 

* 1 credit = 1 email found via [Domain Search](/domain-search), [Email Finder](/email-finder), or Bulk Email Finder
* 1 credit = up to 10 emails found via [Bulk Domain Search](/bulks/domain-search)
* 0.5 credit = 1 email verified via [Email Verifier](/email-verifier) or [Bulk Email Verifier](/bulks/email-verifier)

###

###

### 

In the [Domain Search](/domain-search), 1 credit is used for each email found, while in the Bulk Domain Search, 1 credit is used for up to 10 emails found. If no emails are found, no credits are used.

### 

1 credit is counted for each email found using the [Email Finder](/email-finder). If the email address can't be found, it's free.

### 

0.5 credit is counted per [verified email](/email-verifier). If we fail to verify, no credit is counted; in this case, the verification status returned is Unknown. Hunter does not verify disposable email addresses. They are automatically skipped, and no verification credit is counted.

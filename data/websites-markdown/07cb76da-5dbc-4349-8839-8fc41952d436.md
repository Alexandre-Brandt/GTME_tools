## Frequently Asked Questions

If we don’t answer your question below, please contact us via chat

What is Primeforge, and how does it compare to other tools?

Primeforge is a cold email infrastructure tool that allows you to set up domains and mailboxes specifically for cold outreach at scale. Those are specifically Google Workspace and MS365 mailboxes. As most prospects use those two providers for their emails, this means you can increase your email deliverability by sending from the same providers.

What’s the difference between Primeforge, Mailforge and Infraforge?

Primeforge, Mailforge, and Infraforge each serve different cold email infrastructure needs. Primeforge provides high-quality Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 mailboxes, enabling users to send emails from the same providers most prospects use - maximizing deliverability and inbox placement at scale. Mailforge offers shared cold email infrastructure with automated DNS setup, making it a fast and cost-effective solution for getting started, though with limited control. Infraforge, on the other hand, delivers fully private infrastructure with advanced features such as multi-IP provisioning, custom domain configurations, dedicated IPs, and whitelabel options, ideal for teams or agencies requiring maximum scalability, control, and branding flexibility.

Is there a free trial?

Primeforge is an email infrastructure tool - you need to purchase domains and mailboxes to use it. Unfortunately, this means that we don’t offer a free trial, however, you are free to explore the app without any domains and mailboxes after signing up.

Does Primeforge have automatic DNS set up for my domains?

Yes, Primeforge offers automatic setup of all the technical parameters such as DKIM, DMARC and SPF in accordance with industry best practices. If you have any questions about the technical setup, you can search our [Helpdesk](https://help.salesforge.ai/en/) for the information you need, or reach out to us directly via the chat in the bottom right corner.

What’s included in my subscription?

Our subscription prices include both the automated DNS setup and ongoing hosting and maintenance of your mailboxes and domains.

How many domains & mailboxes do I need?

That depends on the specifics of your sequences. We recommend using our handy calculator that takes into account how many emails you will be sending to each contact and how many contacts you want to process each month. You can find this calculator above, on this website or within the Primeforge app.

What Is The Tenant Setup In Microsoft 365?

In Microsoft 365, a tenant is a dedicated instance of Microsoft’s cloud services. In Primeforge we have 1 tenant per domain.

Can I connect domains I already own to Primeforge?

Yes. You can connect domains you already own to Primeforge.

Click Connect Domain in Primeforge, add your domain, review the connection details (domain connection fee applies), and update the nameservers in your registrar’s dashboard.

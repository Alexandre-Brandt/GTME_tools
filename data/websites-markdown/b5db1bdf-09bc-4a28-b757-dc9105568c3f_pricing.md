## Billing questions

### Am I charged when nothing is found?

Not for contact lookups: they cost credits only when we find data, 15 per mobile number, 5 per email. A mobile or email lookup that returns nothing costs 0 credits. Enrichment, reverse lookups and search are charged per request (1 to 15 credits; the table above lists each one).

### Can I test on my own sample before buying?

Yes. Run your own list on any self-serve plan, or book a demo and send us a sample. Send a list of profiles you care about. We run it and show you what we find, number by number, so you can measure hit rate and connect rate yourself.

### What if a number is wrong?

Report it through the bad-data endpoint and the credits are returned. Right contact every time, or your money back.

### Why are Forager's mobiles more expensive than some alternatives?

Because each one is checked before it reaches you. Validated for connectivity, name match and profile match before it reaches you. A number that rings the wrong person costs you the call, so compare providers on the price of a number that connects to the right person: Forager ranked #1 of 10 in Clay's 2026 test of mobile data providers. If a contact is wrong, report it and the credits are returned.

### Do you offer annual agreements?

Yes, for platforms and enterprise teams. An annual agreement sets a per-record rate that steps down with committed volume, and covers display or resale terms, refresh terms written into the contract, and storage priced separately from per-request use. Data feeds are a flat annual licence, global or regional, not credits. Talk to sales and bring a sample.

### Do Gold and Silver mobiles cost the same?

No. A Gold mobile costs 15 credits and a Silver mobile costs 9. Silver includes every Gold number, and one Silver lookup is charged once even when it returns both. You pay only when a number comes back.

### Can an AI agent or an automated workflow use my account?

Yes. Agents, scheduled jobs and workflows call the same API with your key and are billed the same way: per result found. A mobile or email lookup that returns nothing costs 0 credits. So an agent that retries a lookup and finds nothing costs nothing. For sustained agent volume, talk to sales for volume pricing.

### How is Platforms pricing different?

Platforms embedding Forager pay per record at volume, with pricing that steps down as volume grows, and get display or resale terms. Need a fixed annual fee? Ask us.

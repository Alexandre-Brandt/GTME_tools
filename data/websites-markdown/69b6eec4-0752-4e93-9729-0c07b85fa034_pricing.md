![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6866d7c6ccdb9f9f82800626/687e5c4acae66651e4cbbd08_stars-FFB4B4.svg)

Lucky you! The free trial allows you to test **all the features** available in La Growth Machine, except for custom sequences.

How many leads can I activate per month?

**As many as you want.** La Growth Machine does not limit you in any way.  
  
Since an email or LinkedIn account can only send a limited number of messages a day, it will vary between campaigns.

What are identities?

An identity is a seat for which you can **automate on all three channels** (LinkedIn + Email + Twitter).   
  
Should you need to automate for 2 people, you will need 2 identities

How to add another identity?

You can add another identity (or seat) directly within the app.  
  
**Note:** During your free-trial you only have access to 1 identity, upgrade your account to add multiple identities.

What's the difference between Multichannel Inbox and Rotating Inbox?

The **Multichannel Inbox** is a **unified inbox** that brings together both your **LinkedIn and email conversations** in one place.  
It helps you stay on top of all your multichannel interactions, get full visibility, and never miss a reply — no matter the channel your lead uses.  
  
The **Rotating Inbox**, on the other hand, is all about **sending**.  
It automatically distributes your outbound emails across multiple addresses to **preserve your domain’s deliverability** and **safely scale your outreach volume**.   
  
📷 In short:

* **Multichannel Inbox = manage & read LinkedIn + email messages**
* **Rotating Inbox = send smartly through multiple email addresses**

Add your channels, conditions, delays, and actions as needed — and unleash your creativity to craft the **most effective strategies for your audience**.  
  
📷 It’s the perfect playground for sales teams who want to go beyond ready-made templates.

What's the difference between an invited member (free) and an identity?

**A member** is a person from your company who **has a La Growth Machine account and a widget**. A company can have as many members as specified in each plan (3 for the Basic plan and 25 for the Pro and Ultimate plans).  
  
By inviting a **free member**, they will be able to:

* Import leads, create leads, and prepare campaigns
* Access the account to view campaign performance and qualify leads
* Respond to LinkedIn messages and emails under each identity

**A member cannot launch a campaign.** Their Google and LinkedIn accounts are not connected, so they cannot launch a campaign under their name. To do this, you need to have subscribed to an identity with La Growth Machine. **An identity equals a license.** An identity can then launch campaigns using their profile.   
  
Depending on your plan, **it’s possible to have multiple identities:**

* Up to 3 identities with the Basic plan (3 licenses and therefore 3 billed identities)
* No identity limit for the Pro and Ultimate plansClearer now? Come see for yourself!

How can I send from multiple email addresses?

To scale your volumes while protecting your deliverability, you can connect **multiple sending email addresses per identity**.  
‍  
This is made possible with our **Rotating Inbox**, available starting from the **Pro** plan.  
‍  
Here’s how it works: your campaigns are automatically distributed across multiple inboxes, which helps you:

* Reduce the risk of being flagged
* Protect your domains
* Scale your outreach without stress

With the **Pro** plan, you can connect **up to 5 sending emails per identity**.  
‍  
With **Ultimate**, you go up to **10 per identity**.  
‍  
📷 For example, if your team uses **3 identities** on a **Pro** plan, you can send emails from **up to 15 inboxes in rotation** (5 per identity).

How does billing work with multiple identities?

If you have multiple identities, each identity is billed but the **same billing account** is used for the payment.

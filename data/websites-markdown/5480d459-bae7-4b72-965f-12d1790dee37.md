## Before we get started.

How does the collaboration start?

We start with your business priorities and the work behind them. Together, we identify where to focus, what information is needed, and what a successful first implementation should achieve.

Who implements the solutions?

We work with your teams to bring solutions into real use. Responsibilities, system access, and the scope of the first implementation are agreed together at the start.

What does our team need to contribute?

People who know the work, access to the relevant information, and an owner for the process we improve. Ontora captures knowledge through focused conversations, so discovery can run across teams in parallel.

How do you measure the return?

We agree on the starting point and success measures for each initiative. Depending on the workflow, that can mean processing time, cost per task, capacity, or revenue impact. We assess the result against the effort and cost of implementation.

Where does the Ontora platform fit in?

Your team gets access to Ontora throughout the transformation. It brings together company knowledge, processes, and supporting evidence, giving people and AI a shared foundation for implementation and ongoing improvement.

## Frequently asked questions

How can you predict the nationality of a name?

Many names have their origins in certain countries, creating a strong correlation between names and nationality. With a dataset of ~1 billion people, Nationalize gives statistical probabilities on the most likely nationalities associated with a name. The API returns a ranked list of countries with probability scores.

How accurate is nationality prediction?

Accuracy depends on how distinctive the name is — "Yamamoto" maps cleanly to Japan; "Smith" spreads across many English-speaking countries.

For agent-based workflows, each service exposes an  [MCP server](/documentation/mcp) .

## Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you might want to know before getting started with Nooks.

Can I replace my contact data provider with Nooks?

Most customers maintain their primary data provider. Some people replace their secondary data provider.

Which contact data providers does Nooks integrate with?

Nooks integrates with ZoomInfo, Cognism, and LeadIQ to pass through contact data via API. A subscription with these providers is required in order to access the data in Nooks.

Which contact data providers does Nooks give me access to with the Data package?

Wiza, People Data Labs, Apollo, Forager, Datagma, Prospeo.

Does Nooks replace Clay?

No, Nooks integrates with Clay. Nooks can currently enrich contact data like email and phone numbers.

Can I replace Outreach and Salesloft with Nooks?

Yes. Nooks AI Sequencing fully replaces Outreach and Salesloft.

What CRMs does Nooks integrate with?

Nooks integrates with HubSpot and Salesforce. If you have another CRM, you can use the AI Dialer by uploading a list of contacts via CSV.

Can I replace UserGems and other signal tools with Nooks?

Many companies choose to continue to use their current signal tools and feed that into Nooks while others choose to replace them and consolidate onto Nooks.

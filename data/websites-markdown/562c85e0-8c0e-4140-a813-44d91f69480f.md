## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Jiminny?

Jiminny is a conversation and revenue intelligence platform that captures emails, phone and video calls, provides insights that win deals and syncs them to your CRM. Jiminny brings an end to sales admin, offers crystal-clear visibility, means smarter coaching - and a pipeline you can trust.

### Can I integrate Jiminny with my existing tools?

Yes! Jiminny supports integration with a wide range of tools and platforms. Our flexible APIs allow you to connect with your data sources effortlessly, enabling a smooth workflow.

### How does Jiminny automate tasks?

Jiminny takes the manual work out of managing calls and meetings so your team can focus on what matters most - selling. Our platform automatically records, transcribes, and summarizes conversations, so there’s no need to take notes or manually log activity. Jiminny also syncs data to your CRM, creates follow-up actions, and helps teams track performance and coaching opportunities without extra admin. With automation built into every step - from call capture to actionable insights - Jiminny keeps your workflows smooth, accurate, and fast.

### Is my data secure with Jiminny?

Absolutely. Your data is protected by industry-leading security and privacy standards. We’re SOC 2 Type II certified and fully GDPR, CCPA, and HIPAA compliant, so your information is handled with care and in line with global regulations. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, hosted securely on AWS, and backed by disaster recovery, monitoring, and regular security testing. You’re always in control with SSO, 2FA, and granular permissions and we maintain a 99.9% uptime so your team stays connected, securely.

### What kind of support do you offer?

We’re here to help every step of the way. Jiminny offers dedicated Customer Success as well as Support through live chat, email, and an in-depth Knowledge Base packed with guides and best practices. Our Customer Success team works closely with you to get set up, drive adoption, and maximize value from the platform.

We also provide onboarding sessions, training resources, and ongoing support to ensure your team’s success - not just at launch, but long after.

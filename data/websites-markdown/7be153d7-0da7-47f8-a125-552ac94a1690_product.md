## What is email verification?

Email verification checks whether an address is real and safe to send to. The verifier runs an SMTP handshake with the mail server, without sending a message.

Basic verifiers mark **catch-all domains** as "unknown," so you can't tell if `alex@enterprise.com` is real. LeadMagic's [catch-all validation](/product/catch-all-validation) resolves them, even behind Proofpoint and Mimecast.

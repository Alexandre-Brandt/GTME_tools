## Frequently Asked Questions

Your most pressing questions, answered with clarity.

Who is Surfe best suited for?

Surfe is built for SDRs, BDRs, Account Executives, RevOps teams, recruiters, agencies, and GTM organizations that want to generate more pipeline, improve data quality, and streamline outbound workflows.

What is Surfe's ROI?

Surfe's primary ROI is helping GTM teams generate more qualified pipeline. By combining GTM Intelligence, real-time buying signals, verified contact data, and AI-powered prospect prioritization, Surfe helps teams focus on the accounts and buyers most likely to convert.

Customers also see ROI through higher contact find rates, reduced manual prospecting, cleaner CRM data, and more efficient outbound execution. By automating research, enrichment, and prioritization, Surfe enables sellers to spend less time searching and more time creating pipeline and closing deals.

‍

How accurate is your data, and how often is it refreshed?

Surfe uses multi-source waterfall enrichment and verification technology to maximize accuracy. Contact information is continuously validated across 15+ leading data providers, helping ensure you always work with the most reliable data available.

Unlike traditional databases that rely on periodic refresh cycles, Surfe enriches data in real time. Every search, enrichment, and lookup pulls from multiple sources at the moment you need it, giving you access to the freshest and most complete contact and company data possible.

## Local or Cloud Data Storage?

Same software, same automation, same features. The versions differ in where your workspace data is stored. A Local license can be upgraded to Cloud at any time — the account and its data move over automatically.

### Key Differences

from $8.25 / month

### Data stored locally

All your data stays on your device, in local folders you can access directly.

### Separate data space per LinkedIn account

Each account has its own isolated storage - no data mixing.

### Webhooks

With outgoing webhooks, Linked Helper can send data to third-party systems.

### Always accessible

Data is stored in local folders on your device. No app, no internet required to access your files.

### Access from any device, anywhere

Install the app on a new device and pick up right where you left off.

### Easy data sharing with teammates and clients

Share LinkedIn accounts and campaign data without manual exports.

### Webhooks

With incoming and outgoing webhooks, Linked Helper can receive data from and send data to third-party systems.

### Data reliability

Your data is automatically backed up to the cloud - no risk of losing it if something happens to your device.

### No manual transfers

Switch devices or reinstall the app without moving files manually.

Your additional discount depends on how long a subscription term you pay for upfront.

### 3. How they stack

The two discounts multiply, they are not added up.

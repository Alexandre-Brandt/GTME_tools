# Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

## How does Subskribe pricing work?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/618e7df125a399c15325b172/63c1900f530e9c27c6cfffec_icon-triangle.png)

Pricing depends on a number of factors, including product(s) purchased, term, seats, and more. Please contact our sales team for custom pricing based on your needs.

## How much does Subskribe cost per user?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/618e7df125a399c15325b172/63c1900f530e9c27c6cfffec_icon-triangle.png)

Depending on the package selected, price may vary depending on the number of users you have. Please contact our sales team for custom pricing based on your needs.

## Do integrations cost extra?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/618e7df125a399c15325b172/63c1900f530e9c27c6cfffec_icon-triangle.png)

No, all integrations are included free of charge.

## Can you purchase Subskribe CPQ or Subskribe Billing individually?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/618e7df125a399c15325b172/63c1900f530e9c27c6cfffec_icon-triangle.png)

Yes, you may purchase Subskribe CPQ or Subskribe Billing individually and do not need to purchase the complete Subskribe quote-to-revenue platform.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Digital Sales Room?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/69f79316768ae1249452038e_Screenshot%202025-06-20%20at%20163945%209.png)![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/6a8dbf3f827f78123a89f0e4_minus-icon.png)

One shareable link where buyers and sellers keep everything for a deal, including content, Mutual Action Plans, video, quotes and e-signature, in a single branded space.

Is trumpet secure?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/69f79316768ae1249452038e_Screenshot%202025-06-20%20at%20163945%209.png)![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/6a8dbf3f827f78123a89f0e4_minus-icon.png)

Yes. Password protection on every Pod, SSO and SCIM on Elite, and we complete security questionnaires as part of procurement.

Is trumpet free forever?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/69f79316768ae1249452038e_Screenshot%202025-06-20%20at%20163945%209.png)![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/6a8dbf3f827f78123a89f0e4_minus-icon.png)

The Free plan is free forever and includes 10 Pods per account with unlimited users.

What happens when I hit 10 Pods on the Free plan?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/69f79316768ae1249452038e_Screenshot%202025-06-20%20at%20163945%209.png)![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/6a8dbf3f827f78123a89f0e4_minus-icon.png)

Nothing breaks. Existing Pods stay live, and you will be prompted to move to Pro or Scale for unlimited Pods.

What are Workspaces and who can I add?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/69f79316768ae1249452038e_Screenshot%202025-06-20%20at%20163945%209.png)![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/6a8dbf3f827f78123a89f0e4_minus-icon.png)

Workspaces separate teams, regions or brands under one account, each with their own templates, content and analytics. Available on Elite.

What types of sales cycles can trumpet be used for?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/69f79316768ae1249452038e_Screenshot%202025-06-20%20at%20163945%209.png)![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/6a8dbf3f827f78123a89f0e4_minus-icon.png)

Anything with more than one stakeholder: new business, onboarding, renewals, expansion and partner deals.

Can I integrate my CRM with trumpet Pods?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/69f79316768ae1249452038e_Screenshot%202025-06-20%20at%20163945%209.png)![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/6a8dbf3f827f78123a89f0e4_minus-icon.png)

Yes. Two-way sync with Salesforce and HubSpot is included on every plan, including Free, plus Gong, Slack, DocuSign and PandaDoc higher up the plans.

How do Account Executives use trumpet?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/69f79316768ae1249452038e_Screenshot%202025-06-20%20at%20163945%209.png)![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/6a8dbf3f827f78123a89f0e4_minus-icon.png)

They replace scattered follow-up emails with one Pod per deal, then use engagement signals to see who is actually reading.

How do Customer Success teams use trumpet?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/69f79316768ae1249452038e_Screenshot%202025-06-20%20at%20163945%209.png)![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/6a8dbf3f827f78123a89f0e4_minus-icon.png)

For onboarding plans, QBRs and renewals, with Mutual Action Plans keeping both sides accountable.

What is the benefit of a Digital Sales Room?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/69f79316768ae1249452038e_Screenshot%202025-06-20%20at%20163945%209.png)![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/6a8dbf3f827f78123a89f0e4_minus-icon.png)

Shorter cycles and fewer lost threads: everything in one place, plus data on how buyers engage between calls.

Is there a trumpet mobile app?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/69f79316768ae1249452038e_Screenshot%202025-06-20%20at%20163945%209.png)![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/6a8dbf3f827f78123a89f0e4_minus-icon.png)

There's no separate app to download. Trumpet runs in your browser, so you and your buyers can open and use it on any phone or tablet.

Do buyers need a paid seat?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/69f79316768ae1249452038e_Screenshot%202025-06-20%20at%20163945%209.png)![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/6a8dbf3f827f78123a89f0e4_minus-icon.png)

No. Buyers and customers open Pods for free. You only pay for your own team's seats.

Can I pay monthly or annually?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/69f79316768ae1249452038e_Screenshot%202025-06-20%20at%20163945%209.png)![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/6a8dbf3f827f78123a89f0e4_minus-icon.png)

Both. Pro is $45, Scale $100 and Elite $160 per user per month on monthly billing, or $450, $1,000 and $1,600 per user per year on annual billing.

What's the difference between Pro, Scale and Elite?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/69f79316768ae1249452038e_Screenshot%202025-06-20%20at%20163945%209.png)![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65cf4fecbed2754c22366637/6a8dbf3f827f78123a89f0e4_minus-icon.png)

Pro is for teams of up to 5, with unlimited Pods and HubSpot and Salesforce integration. Scale adds e-signature, proposals, Gong, DocuSign and PandaDoc. Elite adds Copilot, SSO and SCIM, Workspaces, custom domains and a dedicated Customer Success manager.

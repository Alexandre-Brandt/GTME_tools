## FAQs

What is a credit?

A credit is $0.01 of managed work — 100 credits = $1. One pool of credits pays for everything: enrichment, AI columns, managed inboxes and domains, connected accounts, and workflow steps. There is nothing to meter per feature, and every price on this page is shown in credits and in dollars.

What's included in a plan?

There is one plan, OXYGEN, and every rung includes all integrations and native workflows; bringing your own provider keys starts at $99 — the price is simply the credits. OXYGEN runs from $49 to $1,999 a month: $49 buys 4,900 credits, $99 buys 9,900 credits, $199 buys 19,900 credits, $499 buys 52,395 credits, $999 buys 109,890 credits, $1,999 buys 229,885 credits — the $499, $999 and $1,999 rungs include a 5%, 10% and 15% volume bonus, so bigger plans buy more credits per dollar. Your subscription is the credits, so there is no separate platform fee and no shelf-ware like a half-used Instantly plan.

Can I choose a yearly plan and pay monthly?

Yes. Yearly plans charge the full discounted year upfront, saving about 17%. Annual prices are $490.00, $990.00, $1,990.00, $4,990.00, $9,990.00, $19,990.00 before tax. Credits arrive monthly during the paid year. Plans renew yearly unless you cancel; access continues until the paid year ends. Switching from monthly starts at the next paid-period boundary; downgrades and switching to monthly start at year end.

What happens when I run out of credits?

You top up on demand with any 100-credit increment from 800 to 100,000 credits, at $1.30 per 100 credits. Top-ups land in the same pool straight away, and purchased credits never expire.

Do credits expire?

Purchased credits never expire — they stay in the same pool until you spend them. Your current available balance is always visible in Billing.

Is sending really free?

Yes, per message. Email and WhatsApp sends cost 0 credits — you pay for the managed inboxes and connected accounts that send them, never per message, so scaling your send volume never scales your bill.

What do managed inboxes and domains cost?

A managed inbox, Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, is 400 credits ($4) a month together: 300 for the mailbox plus the 100-credit connected-inbox fee every sending mailbox pays, billed separately, with warm-up included. An Azure tenant, a dedicated Microsoft tenant for one domain with up to 100 inboxes, is 4,000 credits ($40) a month, and each inbox on it adds 25 credits ($0.25) a month for its connection. A sending domain is charged at the registrar's price once a year, 1,250 credits ($12.50) for a .com, and holds up to 5 Google or Microsoft inboxes. Deliverability monitoring is optional, 100 credits ($1) per inbox a month with 2 placement tests. Sending from any of them costs nothing per email.

How is WhatsApp priced?

Each connected WhatsApp number reserves 3,400 credits ($34) a month from your balance, the same shape as a LinkedIn account. Messages sent from it cost 0 credits. If you still hold a dollar sending seat, it covers its account until it ends at renewal; from then on the account reserves credits instead, never both.

Is the free tier really free?

Yes, and it has no clock on it. Signing up needs no card: you get the full CRM, Tables, Workflows, Knowledge, and Agents, plus 1,000 credits. You can build a whole outbound motion — sequences, steps, enrollment, previews — before you pay anything. Connecting a sending account (a LinkedIn account, mailbox, WhatsApp number, X account or phone number) and bringing your own provider keys need a plan; publishing and buying domains, warm-up or placement tests only need the credits to pay for them.

How do workflows use credits?

Each step that calls a tool costs 0.05 credits ($0.0005), so 1,000 tool steps cost 50 credits ($0.50), plus the tool's own price, such as an enrichment. Triggers, filters, branches, loops, merges, waits, approvals and code steps are free, and so is writing rows, however many a step writes. A retried step bills again; previews and dry runs cost nothing.

How fast can I call the API?

Rates scale with plan size. Each API key and each workspace may make this many requests a minute: Free 1,200, $49–$99 3,000, $199–$499 6,000, $999 12,000, $1,999 30,000. Table reads, table writes and live enrichment calls have their own per-key limits, listed in the pricing reference. A request over a limit gets HTTP 429 with a Retry-After header that says how long to wait. Limits only guard against runaway loops: they are never sold.

How many webhook deliveries can a table or workflow take?

Each inbound webhook (a table endpoint or a workflow or agent trigger) accepts this many deliveries a minute, by plan size: Free 600, $49–$99 1,500, $199–$499 3,000, $999 6,000, $1,999 12,000. One sender may use the whole rate. A delivery over the limit gets HTTP 429 with a Retry-After header and nothing is written, so the sender redelivers; one delivery can also carry many rows.

What else grows with plan size?

Storage, import file size, the columns one table run can carry and the signals feed. Import file size: Free 500 MiB, $49–$99 1 GiB, $199–$499 2 GiB, $999–$1,999 5 GiB. Columns per table run or standing auto-run: Free 10, $49–$499 25, $999–$1,999 50. Signals feed per read: Free 100 events over 30 days, $49–$99 200 events over 90 days, $199–$499 500 events over 180 days, $999–$1,999 1,000 events over 365 days. The pricing reference and oxygen limits show list every limit for every plan size. Like the API rates, these are guards and never cost credits.

How often can a workflow run?

A scheduled workflow runs as often as every minute on every paid plan, and webhook and event triggers fire on every delivery. A Code step can run for up to 30 seconds and one run attempt for 30 minutes, after which the run picks up from its last checkpoint. Run oxygen limits show to see the fastest schedule on each plan size.

Can I bring my own provider keys?

Yes, on the $99 plan and above. Connect your own enrichment, AI, or provider keys and the provider bills those calls to you directly; OXYGEN adds a platform fee of 0.05 credits ($0.0005) a call, and none on AI calls on your own model key. When OXYGEN fronts the key (managed), it bills in credits at a flat 5× provider cost.

How is LinkedIn priced?

Each connected LinkedIn account reserves 3,400 credits ($34) a month from your balance — invites, messages, InMails, profile visits, follows, likes, and comments all cost 0 additional credits, so the reservation is the whole LinkedIn price. If you run several client workspaces on one plan, the organization that pays holds the reservations. If you still hold a dollar sending seat, it covers its account until it ends at renewal; from then on the account reserves credits instead, never both.

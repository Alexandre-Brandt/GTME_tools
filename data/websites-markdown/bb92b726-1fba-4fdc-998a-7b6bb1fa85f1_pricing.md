# What A Credit Actually Buys

Leadsforge bills per verified result, not per user. Here is what draws down your balance and what does not, so you can size a plan against real usage instead of guessing.

Not every lookup costs the same. Work emails and LinkedIn URLs are the lightest on your balance, while verified mobile numbers cost considerably more, because direct dials are the hardest data to source. If you are modelling monthly spend, phone enrichment is the line that moves it.

## Frequently asked questions

If we don’t answer your question below, please contact us via chat

### What Is Leadsforge, And How Does It Compare To Other Tools?

A lead search engine you describe your buyer to, rather than filter your way through. It sources contacts across multiple providers, verifies them before they reach your list, and hands them off to whatever you use to send. Everything else on this page is about what that costs.

### Is There A Free Trial?

When signing up you will receive 100 free credits to try out Leadsforge risk free.

### What Are Credits?

Anytime you create your custom lead list in Leadsforge, the app will calculate how many credits exporting it will use. This is based on factors such as the number of leads on the list, level of data enrichment etc.

### Do I Have To Use All My Credits Every Month?

No, your Credits don’t expire. If you don’t use them within the billing period they will stay safe on your Leadsforge account.

### Do You Offer A Yearly Discount?

Yes. When purchasing the yearly plan you will save over 14% and get all your credits upfront.

### Do I Have To Commit To A Contract?

No. You can purchase more credits right in the app and cancel your subscription anytime.

### Can You Issue An Invoice?

Yes. After completing the payment, you will receive an invoice for any and all purchases. You can access and edit your billing information in the Leadsforge app.

### What’s Included In My Subscription?

Our subscription plans include Credits that you can use to generate targeted lead lists in Leadsforge. You can then export the lists directly to Salesforge or a CSV file.

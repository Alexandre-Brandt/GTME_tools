## What shapes your quote

Jiminny’s pricing is based on the number and type of seats your team needs. Recording Seats are for reps who join and record calls, Insights Seats are for managers or coaches who review and act on the data, and Listener Seats are for those who just need access to transcripts and summaries.   
  
Our minimum contract length is 12 months, billed monthly or annually at a discounted rate. Unlike our competitors, we don’t charge a platform fee - just a one-time setup fee.

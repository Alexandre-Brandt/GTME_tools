## Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about EmailBison.

Can I send campaigns from multiple email accounts?   

Yes, EmailBison allows teams to connect and manage multiple sending inboxes within workspaces. This makes it easier to organize outbound campaigns across different accounts and clients.

   Can I use EmailBison for client accounts?   

Yes, agencies can use separate workspaces to manage different clients, campaigns, leads, and sending infrastructure. This provides a structured way to keep client operations separated within one platform.

   Does EmailBison support email personalization?   

Yes, EmailBison supports custom variables that can be used to personalize outbound messages. You can use lead information and campaign data to create more relevant email sequences.

   Does EmailBison support A/B testing?   

Yes, EmailBison includes A/B testing capabilities that allow teams to test different versions of their outbound messaging and evaluate campaign performance.

   What happens if I need more than 500,000 emails per month?   

EmailBison is designed to scale beyond its included monthly sending capacity. Teams that require additional volume can add more sending capacity as their outbound requirements grow.

   Can I control when emails are sent?   

Yes, EmailBison provides sending controls that allow users to configure campaign schedules and manage how outbound emails are delivered.

   Does EmailBison charge extra for additional team members?   

No, EmailBison's pricing includes unlimited teammates, so agencies and sales teams can add users without paying a separate per-seat fee.

   Can EmailBison help manage email replies?   

Yes, EmailBison's Master Inbox lets users manage conversations and replies from their outbound campaigns in one centralized interface, making it easier to keep track of prospect interactions.

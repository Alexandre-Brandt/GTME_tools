## Salesforce CRM frequently asked questions

### Is Salesforce a CRM?

Yes. Salesforce CRM offers AI-powered, [customer relationship management (CRM)](https://www.salesforce.com/crm/what-is-crm/) software that makes it easier for companies to build customer relationships, be more productive, and increase efficiency. This helps businesses of all sizes and in all industries conect with customers and prospects at scale.  
  
Salesforce CRM brings together all your data onto [one integrated platform](https://www.salesforce.com/products/) that unites your [sales](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/), [service](https://www.salesforce.com/service/), [marketing](https://www.salesforce.com/marketing/), [commerce](https://www.salesforce.com/commerce/), and [IT teams](https://www.salesforce.com/platform/) with a single, shared view of your customer.

### Is Salesforce an online CRM?

Yes. Salesforce is an online CRM, also known as a [cloud-based CRM](https://www.salesforce.com/crm/cloud-crm/). It is a [Software as a Service (SaaS)](https://www.salesforce.com/saas/) platform, which delivers applications over the internet. As a cloud-based CRM system, all platform hosting and maintenence is performed by Salesforce, eliminating many of the costs and responsibilities involved in maintaining CRM infrastructure and security management.

### How much does Salesforce CRM cost?

There are a variety of Salesforce [CRM pricing](https://www.salesforce.com/crm/pricing/) options. You can also view all [Salesforce product pricing here](https://www.salesforce.com/pricing/).

### What types of businesses use Salesforce?

Salesforce is built to be a scalable and [customizable CRM](https://www.salesforce.com/crm/custom-crm/), so that it can meet the needs of businesses across all sizes, stages, and industries. Salesforce provides [enterprise CRM](https://www.salesforce.com/products/) options, as well as [CRM for small businesses](https://www.salesforce.com/small-business/) and [CRM for startups](https://www.salesforce.com/crm/startup-crm/).

### Why do businesses need CRM if they already have AI?

AI models are powerful but don’t know a company’s customers, business rules, or permissions. CRM fills those gaps with trusted customer data, the workflows that encode how the business runs, and the governance (permissions, guardrails, and audit trails) that controls and traces what agents can see and do. CRM isn’t replaced by AI; it’s what makes AI reliable, accountable, and useful at enterprise scale.

### How easy is it to use Salesforce?

Salesforce was built to be easy-to-use by all employees across your entire organization.

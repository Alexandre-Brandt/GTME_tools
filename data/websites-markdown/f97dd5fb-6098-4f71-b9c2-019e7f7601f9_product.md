## Quick answers about Email Verifier

Here are the most common ones we get.

### Is email verification accurate? How reliable is an email checker?

Findymail verifies emails in real-time at the moment you submit them, not against an outdated database. We guarantee a bounce rate under 5%. If your bounce rate exceeds 5%, contact support for a bounce analysis and we'll refund you. [Independent testing by Clay](https://www.clay.com/data-tests/best-work-email-provider-by-region-2025) ranked us #1 for email accuracy with 23% more valid emails than competitors.

### What are catch-all emails and why does it matter?

Some domains (common at enterprise companies) don't confirm whether an email exists—these are called catch-all domains. Most tools mark these emails as "risky" or "unknown" and tell you not to use them. That means you lose 30% of your addressable market. Findymail has advanced verification that validates catch-all emails other tools give up on, so you can reach more decision-makers.

### Can I verify emails for free?

Yes. You can try the email verifier for free when you sign up, no credit card required. After that, email verification is included in every plan.

### Does checking emails send messages to recipients?

No. Email verification checks whether an email address is valid without sending any messages. Recipients won't know their email was verified.

### How long does email verification take?

Verification happens in real-time. Small lists (hundreds of emails) verify in seconds. Larger lists (thousands of emails) take a few minutes depending on size. We clean thousands of emails in minutes, even at scale.

### Is bulk email verification possible? How many emails can I check?

Yes. You can verify lists of any size by uploading a CSV or XLS file. You can validate thousands of email addresses per month depending on your plan.

### Does your email checker work with CRM and marketing tools?

Yes. Findymail integrates natively with HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Instantly, Smartlead, Lemlist, and more. You can also connect via Zapier, Make, or our API to build email address verification into forms, sign-up workflows, or contact imports.

### How does email verification help with email deliverability?

Sending to invalid or risky emails increases your bounce rate, which damages your sender reputation and causes your emails to land in spam. Verifying emails before you send ensures you only contact valid addresses, keeping your bounce rate low and your sender reputation clean.  
  
Our verifier removes:

*   invalid or fake emails
*   temporary/disposable email addresses
*   risky and role-based addresses that affect delivery

The result: more emails that reach the inbox.

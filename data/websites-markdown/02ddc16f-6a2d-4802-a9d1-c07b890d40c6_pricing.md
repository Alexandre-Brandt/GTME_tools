## Frequently Asked Questions

Who is a user?

   

A user is anyone that will be using the system: an agent, supervisor or administrator. When creating a user, you can choose to associate this new user with an extension or not.

   

Can I change my subscription at anytime?

   

Yes! You are free to switch plans, add and remove users, upgrade or downgrade, at any time.

   

Can I use my extension on my desktop and smartphone?

   

Yes! You can configure your credentials on up to 3 separate devices: desktop app, mobile app and IP phone. When you receive an incoming call, all devices connected with your credentials will ring simultaneously.

   

Are your prices excluding taxes?

   

Yes, all the Business Phone Pricing and call rates are excluding taxes.

Can I mix users and plans?

   

No, but if you have a particular requirement, we recommend speaking with one of our account managers to find the best Business Phone Pricing solution for your needs.

   

Can I port my existing phone number over?

   

In most cases, yes! If you own a phone number with your current provider, you can port it over to your NUACOM Account. Also, we can set up a call divert from your existing provider to ensure there will be no downtime during the switch. For more information on the porting process, timeframe and charges, please contact us.

   

How long is the contract?

   

We offer a 30-day rolling contract basis for all of our standard business phone pricing. If you are interested in an annual contract, please contact our Sales Team or use our [live chat](#open-chat).

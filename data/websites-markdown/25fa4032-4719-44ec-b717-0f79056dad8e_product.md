## How do you want to set up paid messaging?

Connect a number, draft a message, and start sending. Pick the setup that fits your team.

#### Partner integration

Pick a partner and onboard through their embedded signup — they handle the technical setup, little or no code required.

Best if you:

* Don’t want to manage the API yourself
* Want to be up and running quickly
* Prefer a partner to onboard and support you

[Browse partners](https://business.facebook.com/messaging/partner-showcase?ref=WAB_BUSINESS_PLATFORM)

#### Direct integration

Connect directly to the WhatsApp Business Platform and send from your own phone number.

Best if you:

* Have your own phone number to send
* Have developers who can integrate the WhatsApp Business Platform
* Want full control of the experience to onboard and support you

[Start direct setup](https://developers.facebook.com/products/whatsapp/onboard/direct)

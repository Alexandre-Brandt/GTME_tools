# How the Talon.One platform works

Disconnected discounts drain margins. Basic loyalty programs fall flat.  
Talon.One gives you the tools to unify promotions and loyalty programs into   
one strategy, and turn customer rewards into results.

Enterprise Loyalty Management

## Bring loyalty to life

Modern program managementRun any imaginable loyalty program securely and consistently across channels - online and in-store.

Engage beyond earn & burn  
Use point promotions, cart-native loyalty, and gamified challenges to drive repeat behavior and higher AOV.

Boost profitability with personalization  
Connect to customer data and engagement tools to deliver personalized rewards.

Offer Management & Execution

## Make discounts count

Go beyond cart & item discounts  
Build bundles, referrals, and product-level offers that display value in real-time and increase AOV.

Manage complexity with confidence  
Govern thousands of promotional campaigns with budgets, fraud protection, and precise evaluation logic.

Perform at enterprise scale  
Execute millions of secure decisions per minute with sub-50 ms response times and global reliability.

## Got questions?

Talon.One is the most powerful incentives engine that unifies loyalty, promotions and gamification into one holistic platform.

Backed by enterprise-grade security and scalability, Talon.One empowers companies to build personalized, profitable promotions and loyalty programs using any data. The world’s most-loved brands including Adidas, Nordstrom, Sephora and Carlsberg work with Talon.One to drive deeper engagement and lasting loyalty with their customers.

Talon.One was founded in 2015. 10 years later, the company has a global reach with teams in Berlin, London, Boston and Singapore, and over 270 clients across North America, Europe and APAC.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a3a5a00bbced045110f411f/6a87c4bbcbcbd01da7fc9f70_TitanX_AI%20Icon.svg)![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a3a5a00bbced045110f411f/6a87c5061c3abca6ec0f4fd4_X%20-%20Ai.svg)

TitanX is priced per seat. For a minimum of three reps, paid annually, Standalone Phone Intent™ scoring into your own dialer starts at $375 per seat per month, and the Precision Dialer seats start at $475 per seat per month. One seat costs less than a new SDR, and less than the data, dialer, number-management and coaching stack it replaces. [Use our calculator](/resources/calculator) to see how the math works on your team.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a3a5a00bbced045110f411f/6a87c4bbcbcbd01da7fc9f70_TitanX_AI%20Icon.svg)![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a3a5a00bbced045110f411f/6a87c5061c3abca6ec0f4fd4_X%20-%20Ai.svg)

TitanX is priced per seat, and comes with a pool of credits shared across the account to be used for Phone Intent scoring and enrichment. Each seat includes a monthly allotment of Phone Intent scoring credits, and you can buy more at any time, so cost scales with production rather than with how carefully reps ration a balance. Seat-based pricing means the question is which reps deserve a seat, not how many credits to spend.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a3a5a00bbced045110f411f/6a87c4bbcbcbd01da7fc9f70_TitanX_AI%20Icon.svg)![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a3a5a00bbced045110f411f/6a87c5061c3abca6ec0f4fd4_X%20-%20Ai.svg)

Yes — some TitanX customers score first, see the lift, and move to the full Precision Dialer later. Phone Intent on your own dialer beats no Phone Intent on your own dialer. What it does not solve is execution, and it does not protect the numbers you call from: without Titan Defense those numbers can still get spam-flagged. You also go without Smart Stream, reporting and analytics, campaign management, coaching and feedback features, and leaderboards.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a3a5a00bbced045110f411f/6a87c4bbcbcbd01da7fc9f70_TitanX_AI%20Icon.svg)![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a3a5a00bbced045110f411f/6a87c5061c3abca6ec0f4fd4_X%20-%20Ai.svg)

Yes — TitanX sells international seats and number packages separately from North American ones, and we confirm coverage for your geographies during the pilot. Number provisioning rules differ from country to country, which is why international dialing is quoted rather than listed. Bring your target geographies to the [demo](/demo) and we’ll price it out with you.

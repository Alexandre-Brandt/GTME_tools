## AI Voice Agent FAQs

### What is an AI voice agent?

An AI voice agent is an autonomous system that can listen, speak, and hold natural phone conversations like a human. It uses speech recognition, large language models, and text-to-speech technology to understand callers, respond intelligently, maintain context, and take actions such as booking appointments or updating systems without relying on rigid scripts or human intervention.

### Which is the best AI voice agent?

The best AI voice agent depends on what you need it to do. For production phone calls, evaluate platforms on four things: workflow accuracy on multi-step calls, latency under real network conditions, how much of the agent logic you control, and whether you can test on your own traffic before committing. In an independent Cekura benchmark across 414 calls, Retell AI scored 95.7% workflow accuracy, the highest of six platforms tested. See how Retell AI [compares to other platforms.](https://www.retellai.com/comparisons/competitors-overview)

### How much do AI voice agents cost?

Retell AI runs $0.07 to $0.31 per minute for voice agents depending on the models, voice, and add-ons you select, with no annual contract and no per-resolution charge. Chat agents start at $0.002 per message. You can [estimate your cost](https://www.retellai.com/pricing) on the pricing page, or [compare enterprise plans.](https://www.retellai.com/enterprise-plan)

### Do the AI voice agents sound like a real person?

Most people cannot tell on a short call. Voice quality comes down to three things: the underlying speech model, response latency, and how the agent handles interruptions. Retell AI agents handle mid-sentence interruptions and natural turn-taking rather than waiting for a full pause, and can transfer to a human mid-call when a conversation needs one. The fastest way to judge it is to hear one, [so try the live demo.](https://www.retellai.com/features/call-transfer)

### Is using AI voice illegal?

No, using AI voice agents is legal in the United States, but it is regulated. Outbound calling requires prior consent under the TCPA, some states require disclosure that the caller is automated, and call recording consent rules vary by state. The FCC has ruled that AI-generated voices in robocalls fall under existing TCPA restrictions. Compliance depends on how the platform is used, not on the platform itself. ### What is an AI call center?

An AI call center uses AI phone agents to handle customer calls instead of routing every interaction to a person. The agent answers, understands what the caller wants, resolves routine requests, and escalates complex ones to a human with full context attached.

### How do AI call centers actually function?

AI call centers layer four technologies into one pipeline: a voice engine that converts speech to text and back in ~600ms, intent detection that identifies caller needs and pulls CRM history instantly, telephony APIs (Twilio, Vonage, SIP) that plug into your existing phone system, and post-call automation that logs data, creates tickets, and triggers follow-ups without human intervention.

### Can AI replace call center agents?

No. AI phone agents handle repetitive, high-volume work like appointment booking, order status, and qualification, which frees your team for calls that need judgment or empathy. Most Retell AI deployments run a hybrid model: the voice agent takes the routine volume and hands off to a person when the conversation calls for it.

### What can a Retell AI voice agent do on a call?

It answers inbound calls and places outbound ones, qualifies leads, books appointments, checks order status, and routes or transfers to a live agent when needed. Mid-call, it can pull and push data through your existing systems, so it acts on real customer information rather than reading from a script.

### What are the main benefits of using an AI call center?

Shorter wait times, calls answered 24/7, lower cost per call, and capacity that scales with volume instead of headcount. Because the agent handles routine calls end to end, average handle time drops and your team spends its hours on higher-value conversations.

### How does Retell AI improve performance and quality assurance?

Every call is transcribed and available for review, so you evaluate 100 percent of interactions instead of a manual sample. Transcripts and call data surface recurring issues, drop-off points, and coaching opportunities across your full call volume, not a handful of spot checks.

### Does Retell AI’s AI call center offer multilingual support?

Yes. Voice agents can hold conversations in multiple languages, so you serve customers in their preferred language without staffing separate multilingual teams.

### How does Retell AI connect to my existing tools?

Retell AI integrates with CRMs, calendars, and telephony through native connectors and APIs. The agent reads customer context before and during the call and writes outcomes back automatically, so records stay current without manual entry.

### How is an AI phone agent different from traditional IVR?

Traditional IVR forces callers through fixed menus most people try to skip. A Retell AI voice agent understands natural speech, lets the caller say what they need in their own words, resolves simple requests directly, and routes the rest without menu trees.

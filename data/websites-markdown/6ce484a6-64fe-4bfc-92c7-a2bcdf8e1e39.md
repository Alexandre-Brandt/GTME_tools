## Security and compliance built into every call

Every call your team makes carries customer data. CloudTalk protects it with security controls independently audited against SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001, strong encryption, and privacy safeguards that support GDPR, CCPA and HIPAA requirements.

### Independently audited

ISO 27001 certified and SOC 2 Type II attested. Independent auditors verify our controls every year, so your vendor review starts with the evidence already in hand.

### Privacy and GDPR compliance

We process your data in line with GDPR, with a Data Processing Agreement included as standard. International transfers stay compliant through Standard Contractual Clauses and our EU-US Data Privacy Framework certification.

### Your data, encrypted and secure on AWS

Your data is stored in secure, independently audited AWS data centers, and every call and recording is encrypted in transit and at rest with TLS and AES-256.

### Access control

Sign in through your existing identity provider, and use role-based permissions to give every admin, supervisor and agent access to exactly what they need.

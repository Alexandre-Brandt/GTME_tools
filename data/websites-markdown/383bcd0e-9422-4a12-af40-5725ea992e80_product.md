## Frequently asked questions

### What is intelligent iPaaS?

An intelligent iPaaS is an integration platform with AI built into how integrations are built and run, not added later: AI-assisted building, document processing, and AI agents that use the same connectors and governance as your workflows. Tray.ai's Intelligent Integration runs process automation, data integration, API management and IDP on one platform, alongside Merlin Agent Builder and Agent Gateway for MCP.

### How does this compare to Workato or Boomi?

Workato has a polished recipe builder and a broad library, and it suits teams that want a shared recipe model. Boomi has real depth in SAP, Oracle and EDI, with an Atom runtime that runs on your own infrastructure. Intelligent Integration runs integration, automation and AI agents in one codebase, with no runtime to operate and no per-user or per-connection fees. For mid-market teams running NetSuite or another ERP, the Boomi alternatives guide covers the options at /blog/boomi-alternatives/, and /blog/best-ipaas-governance/ compares nine platforms on built-in governance. Full comparisons at /compare/.

### Can we keep our existing automations if we migrate?

Yes. Most customers migrate incrementally — keep legacy workflows running while building new ones on Tray.ai. Yext moved 100+ services off MuleSoft in 3 months at 60% lower cost.

### What scale can it handle?

Cisco runs 600M+ tasks a year across 60+ automations. DocuSign runs 150M tasks a month. Auctane processes 3B orders a year. We don't run out of headroom.

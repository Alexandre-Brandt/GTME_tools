## Questions, answered.

Which plan is right for my team?

If you want to source swag, start with Merch. If you want to get gifts to key contacts fast, start with Gifting. If you want gifting to be a repeatable team play, go Core. If you want to scale across teams and regions with AI, go Advanced. If you need enterprise governance, go Enterprise.

What's the difference between Start with Merch and Start with Gifting?

Both include 5 licenses and no spend commitment. Merch is built around sourcing and shipping branded swag. Gifting adds marketplace sends, global eGifts, and a platform integration.

Is there a spend commitment?

No. Every plan comes with no spend commitment required and low minimum order quantities on custom merch.

Can I just order swag without a plan?

Yep. Head to merch.sendoso.com to create a custom merch order with no platform fee.

Why isn't pricing listed?

Plans are tailored to your team size, sending volume, and regions. Talk to our team and we'll put together a quote that fits.

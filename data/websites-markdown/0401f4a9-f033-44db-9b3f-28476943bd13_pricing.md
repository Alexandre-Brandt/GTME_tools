## Pricing FAQs

##### What is a workspace in Planable?

A workspace is where your team creates, reviews, approves, and schedules content for a specific brand or client. It keeps everything for that brand in one place, including social media pages, campaigns, collaborators, and shared assets.

Workspaces let you **separate clients and brands**, keep collaboration **private**, and **customize workflows** so each client relationship runs the way you need.

##### How many workspaces do I need?

Planable recommends **one workspace per brand or client**.

* If you manage **5 clients**, you need **5 workspaces**.
* If one brand operates across **3 regions** with separate teams, you need **3 workspaces**.

##### What are the workspace settings?

Each workspace has its own setups and workflow controls, including:

* Timezone settings
* Auto-schedule posts after approval
* Lock content once approved
* Approval, notification, and publishing preferences
* Timetable with preferred posting time slots
* Custom labels
* Post templates

##### How do the 50 free posts work?

Planable has a free trial that gives you 50 created posts total, with no credit card required. The trial has **no time limit**, and it’s designed to let you test Planable with real workflows before upgrading.

The 50 posts don’t reset monthly. Once they’re used, you’ll need to upgrade to keep publishing.

During the free trial, you don’t have access to X (Twitter) publishing.

Analytics and Social inbox add-ons can only be tested on a paid plan, but each add-on includes a 30-day free trial.

##### What does the Enterprise plan include?

Planable’s Enterprise plan is designed for teams managing content at scale, especially when approval complexity, security requirements, and fast support are critical.

Enterprise includes everything on the Pro plan, plus:

* Unlimited social media platforms and posts
* Multi-level approval workflows
* All content viewing options: feed, calendar, grid, and list (for bulk actions)
* Unlimited labels and campaigns
* 100 GB storage for your media assets
* SSO integration
* Dedicated account manager
* Personalized onboarding
* Priority support
* 24 months of published posts storage
* Custom pricing based on your team’s setup
* Analytics and Social inbox add-ons are available

If you’re managing 5+ workspaces or need volume pricing, [get in touch](https://planable.io/book-a-demo/) to discuss Enterprise.

##### What add-ons does Planable offer?

Planable offers two add-ons, available on all paid plans. Both add-ons are priced per workspace, and you can enable or disable them at the workspace level. Each add-on includes a free 30-day trial.

**[Analytics add-on](https://planable.io/analytics/) ($14/workspace/month)**

Includes cross-channel performance data, page and post-level metrics, audience insights, and customizable client-ready reports.

**[Social inbox add-on](https://planable.io/engagement/) ($9/workspace/month)**

Includes a unified inbox for comments and DMs across all connected pages, with AI-suggested replies and sentiment filtering.

Both can be added or removed at the workspace level, and come with a free 30-day free trial.

##### What payment methods does Planable accept?

Planable accepts all major debit and credit cards. Payments are made securely via Stripe, which meets and exceeds industry standards in security.

For Enterprise plans, Planable can also support bank or wire transfer, which is useful for teams with more complex procurement requirements.

##### Is Planable GDPR compliant?

Yes. Planable’s [Terms of service](https://planable.io/terms/), [Privacy policy](https://planable.io/privacy/), and [Cookie policy](https://planable.io/cookies/) reflect GDPR regulations.

##### Does Planable offer discounts for non-profits?

Yes. Reach out to our support team at [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#a1d2d4d1d1ced3d5e1d1cdc0cfc0c3cdc48fc8ce) to get more details.

##### Can I change or cancel my plan at any time?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from your Billing Dashboard by selecting Cancel subscription.

If you’re on an annual plan and cancel mid-cycle, you’ll keep access until the end of your current billing period.

If you’re on a custom plan created by our sales team, changes are handled through your dedicated account manager and follow your custom, pre-agreed terms.

##### What social media platforms does Planable support?

Planable supports **content scheduling** for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, Google Business Profile, and Threads.

**Planable Analytics** is available for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, and Google Business Profile.

**Planable Social inbox** covers **comments** for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, and **DMs** for Facebook and Instagram.

Beyond social media, Planable’s universal content lets you plan, review and approve any type of content, including newsletters, blog posts, ads, other non-social content your team produces.

##### How is Planable different from other social media management tools?

Most social media tools treat collaboration as an afterthought. In Planable, it’s the foundation.

Planable is collaboration-first, so your team can **create, review, and approve content in one place** before anything goes live. That means fewer scattered files, fewer back-and-forth messages, and a clearer approval process.

Planable is designed for:

* **Agencies** managing multiple clients and approval cycles
* **Brands** coordinating internal teams plus external stakeholders (partners, legal, executives)

With Planable, you can:

* Handle multiple brands in dedicated workspaces
* Plan content in the intuitive drag-and-drop calendar
* Create and refine pieces with comments, annotations, and suggestions
* Approve content in your personalized workflow: from none required to multi-approval levels
* Directly publish to the major social media platforms
* Engage with your community via comments & DMs
* Analyze your social media performance
* Quickly create reports for your stakeholders and clients

Planable supports **internal posts and internal comments**, so your team can draft, edit, and approve content privately. You can keep work-in-progress versions hidden, then share the final post with clients only when it’s ready.

This avoids duplicate drafts and messy version names like “v2,” “final,” and “final-final.” Everything stays in one place, with a clear review trail.

Planable’s **pricing is per workspace, with unlimited users**. It’s not priced per seat. That makes it a better fit for teams that collaborate, review, and approve content together, without extra costs as more people join.

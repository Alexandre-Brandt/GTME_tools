## The foundation you don't have to build.

![Context Graph](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/67c1b050b877e54850784b35/6acee1fa38fe3334d411ecb0_context-graph-atom.svg)

### Context Graph

#### A living model of your GTM strategy.

Octave’s purpose-built GTM ontology models your products, segments, personas, use cases, motions, competitors, and much more. All your GTM knowledge in one place.  
  
It captures the intricate relationships between what you sell, who you sell to, and how you differentiate, giving your tools and agents the exact context most relevant to the task at hand.

[Learn more](/platform/context-graph)

![Intelligence Engine](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/67c1b050b877e54850784b35/6acee1fa1699213d9fef3325_intelligence-engine.svg)

### Intelligence Engine

#### A recursive loop that learns what wins.

Octave finds the winning patterns that you don’t know to look for. It inspects every deal, call, email, and interaction to learn exactly what's driving wins and sharpen your team's edge.  
  
Go beyond flat ICP definitions to understand the nuanced pains, pressures, and mindset of every business and buyer you encounter. It learns autonomously so you stay in sync with your market.

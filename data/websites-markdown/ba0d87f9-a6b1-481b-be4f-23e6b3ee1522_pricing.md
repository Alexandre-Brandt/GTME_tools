## Frequently asked **questions**

Everything about Cello, from programs to channels and pricing.

Which products are eligible for Cello?

Cello works with software products that offer a logged-in web or mobile experience. Your users must be able to authenticate, as our referral technology requires an in-app environment to function.

What if my product is not eligible at this point in time?

Cello requires a logged-in web or mobile app experience, as our technology needs an authenticated environment. If your product doesn’t support this yet, we’re unfortunately not able to onboard you at this time. If you introduce a logged-in experience in the future, we’d be happy to explore Cello with you again.

How do you define referral ARR and how is it calculated?

Referral ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue) is an annualized revenue success metric for a referral program. To calculate the Referral ARR, Cello examines the realized, effective recurring revenue that came in via Cello each month and annualizes it. For monthly subscriptions: Cello takes the monthly recurring revenue (MRR) and multiplies it by 12 to project the annual value. For yearly subscriptions: Cello takes the annual subscription value directly as the ARR contribution for that period.

If paying customers acquired through Cello that pay less or fully churn, will this reduce the referral ARR?

Referral ARR takes all account-based revenue changes into account. If a customer of your product that was acquired via Cello referrals expands or downgrades the monthly subscription with you, this will change the Revenue ARR accordingly. Churned customers will be completely taken out from the monthly Referral ARR calculation.

Is there a minimum commitment period for each plan?

Monthly plans can be canceled at any time. Yearly plans come with a 12-month commitment.

Are there any hidden fees not included in the pricing—such as setup costs, currency conversion or payment transaction fees?

There are no setup or currency conversion fees. User Referrals have 0% transaction fees. Partner Referral transaction fees vary by tier: Free 5%, Plus 3.5%, Pro 3.5%, and Enterprise 2%.

What does the billing cycle look like?

Cello will be billing and charging your credit card up to twice a month in order to ensure timely reward payouts.

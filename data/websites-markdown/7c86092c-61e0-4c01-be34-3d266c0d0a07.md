**How Kiflo works**

## Kiflo holds the truth. Your AI does the work.

### Your AI sees everything. Kiflo holds the data.

Ask which partners went quiet, which deals are stuck and what partners brought in this quarter. Get the table or the board update in seconds. Every partner, deal and activity lives in Kiflo, synced two-way with HubSpot and Salesforce.

### Your AI does the work. Kiflo keeps it right.

Chase partners, push stalled deals to close, create tasks and register deals. Draft partner emails, one-pagers and content from live program data. Kiflo applies your rules and syncs every change to your CRM.

### Your AI settles who gets paid and why. Kiflo makes the numbers hold.

Ask which partner sourced a deal and what you owe this month, with the history to prove it. Kiflo applies your rules and calculates attribution and commissions, so you validate in minutes.

Everything a partner program needs

## A complete PRM, built to be run by your AI.

### Revenue Tracking

Partner-sourced and partner-influenced revenue by partner and tier, in Kiflo and in your HubSpot reports.

### Commission Automation

Commissions calculated automatically, renewals through Stripe or Chargebee included, with self-invoicing and payout statements. Kiflo calculates, your finance team pays.

### Partner Portal

A branded space where partners refer leads with a passwordless form and see their deal status and earnings. Unlimited partner users, free for partners.

### Partner Management

Applications, onboarding steps, tier qualification, enablement, channel conflict detection and two-tier distribution.

### Partner Directory

Built from your Kiflo partner data, white-label and embedded on your site. Buyers contact a partner from their profile, and Kiflo creates the lead, routes it and syncs it to your CRM.

### Account Mapping and Co-Selling

Find overlaps with the accounts you want to win using AI overlap detection, then turn them into intros and shared deals with a "Request partner influence" button on HubSpot deals.

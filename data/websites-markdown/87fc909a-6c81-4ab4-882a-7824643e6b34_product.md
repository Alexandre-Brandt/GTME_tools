## Integrations

![](/data/files/EQWBPXH4472HFWPB/dialfire-integration.png)

### CRM Systems

Dialfire integrates with many popular CRM systems and is listed as a partner in their marketplaces. These connectors are bidirectional, meaning you primarily create and manage your campaigns in your CRM system while using Dialfire for telephony. All results are automatically synced back to your CRM system.

If your CRM system isn't directly supported, it's likely compatible with our Zapier connector, and if not, integration through existing APIs is always an option.

### BI-Tools

Dialfire has a direct connector to the popular Looker Studio, allowing you to access the same reporting metrics as within Dialfire.

Alternatively, you can use the DBSync adapter to synchronize all data in real-time with a local SQL database. This allows you to use locally installed BI tools of your choice.

### Calendars

Appointment scheduling is a common use case for Dialfire users. With our calendar connectors, you can schedule directly into the calendars of your employees or entire field service organizations. In the latter case, you also benefit from integrated travel time planning and optimization, avoiding zigzag routes through the sales territory.

Record calls by default or on-demand with a single click. Calls can be recorded one-way, two-way, or both. We keep the recordings available for at least 3 years unless you delete them earlier.

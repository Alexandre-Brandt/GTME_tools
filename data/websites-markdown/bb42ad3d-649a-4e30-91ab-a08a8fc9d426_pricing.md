Maildoso uses monthly pay-per-mailbox pricing.  
  
As of June 2026 the plans are:  

* 30 mailboxes for $75/month ($2.5 per mailbox),
* 300 mailboxes for $225/month ($0.75 per mailbox),
* and 1,000 mailboxes for $499/month ($0.49 per mailbox).

If you need a specific number of mailboxes (outside of standard subscription plans), you can purchase them on the ['Packages & Add-ons' page](https://app.maildoso.com/billing/packages-addons) inside the platform (prices start at $0.49).  
  
Every plan includes self-healing mailboxes, IP rotation, CAPTCHA domain protection, API and MCP access, free deliverability audits, and a a deliverability guarantee — if your deliverability rate falls below 80%, we’ll refund your money..

We offer SMTP mailboxes designed specifically for outbound campaigns.  
  
**Advantages of SMTP:**  

* Designed specifically for outbound
* Lower cost (starting from $0.49)
* Fully proprietary infrastructure, including servers and IPs
* Your campaigns are sent from different IPs, and we automatically replace them with new ones if they stop working
* We automatically replace links and images in your emails with safe alternatives using Global Customer Domain Tracking
* Burned-out mailboxes are automatically disconnected from campaigns for 14 days to recover
* We run inbox placement tests every 3 days and display up-to-date health scores for each mailbox.

Learn more [on the SMTP product page](/product/smtp); all the packages are available [on the Pricing page](/pricing).  
  
**Can I delete mailboxes?**   
Yes.  
  
You purchase a license for a specific number of mailboxes rather than specific accounts. If you delete and recreate them, you don't need to pay extra. However, please note that new mailboxes require a warm-up period.  
  
**How do I change the names of mailboxes?**   
Find out in our [Knowledge Base](https://intercom.help/maildoso/en).

Are domains included in the plans?  
Yes, but only for quarterly SMTP plans. For monthly plans, they must be purchased separately, or you can connect your existing domains.  
  
How much do domains cost?  
The domain registration fee is $15 per year. This price is fixed and does not change based on the number of domains.  
  
You can purchase domains on [the pricing page](/pricing) or in your personal account on the [Packages & Add-ons](https://app.maildoso.com/billing/packages-addons) page.  
  
Which TLD's are available for domain registration?  
We use the following TLDs for domain registration: .com, .net, .org, .one, .co, .email, .ink, .digital, .company, .pro, .services, .space, .co.uk.

Do I retain ownership of domains purchased through Maildoso?  
While you are using our service, domains are registered with us, enabling us to facilitate processes such as creating emails, IP rotation, and more.  
You can request that the domains be returned at any time, and we will transfer them back to you at no charge at the end of your subscription.  
  
How many mailboxes can I create per domain?  
Up to 99 mailboxes per domain, but we recommend no more than 8.

How many emails can I send from one mailbox?  
You can send up to 15 cold emails / 80 warm-up emails per day, no slow ramp-up (*clarify if your sequencer allows for this volume of warm-up emails).*

Thank you for choosing us! Here are your next steps:  
  
1) Authorization  
For some of our plans, you need to authorize first — in this case, you can log in immediately, and your mailbox license will already be available.  
If you didn't authorize during checkout, your login credentials will be sent to the email address used in Stripe. Check your spam folder if you don't see them.  
  
2) Connecting Domains  
If you have your own domains, send the list via chat or to [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection); they will be connected within 24 hours. If you don't have domains, you can buy them for $12/year on our [pricing page](/pricing) or on the [Packages & Add-ons page](https://app.maildoso.com/billing/packages-addons).  
  
3) Registering Mailboxes  
Go to the 'Accounts' page and click 'Add more email accounts'.  
Enter the first and last names, and select the domain. You can auto-fill multiple lines at once.  
Need more than 4? Click 'Add new line'.  
  
4) Connecting to your Sequencer  
Select the accounts, click 'Export selected to CSV', and choose your format (we support 9 sequencers natively).  
More info [here](https://intercom.help/maildoso/en).  
  
How long does it take?  

* Registering a new domain: up to 15 minutes.
* Adding an existing domain: up to 24 hours.
* Registering a mailbox: 5 minutes.

  
5) Warm-up  
For any new mailboxes and domains, you must run a warm-up for at least 14 days before launching your first campaign. Keep warm-up active for the entire lifespan of the mailboxes, using the maximum volume possible — you can combine different providers or use the Instantly.ai warm-up pool.  
*Warning: Skipping warm-up prevents your mailboxes from building domain reputation and almost always results in them burning out.*

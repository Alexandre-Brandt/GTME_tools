General Questions

### Which Shopware plan is right for my business?

Choose based on the capabilities and support your business needs. Community Edition gives you an open-source foundation to build and customize your store. Rise adds AI-assisted content creation, product customization and returns management, with direct support from Shopware. Evolve adds B2B buying capabilities such as quotes and purchasing approvals, advanced search and content targeting for customer groups. Beyond adds customer-specific pricing from external systems, inventory across multiple locations and Digital Sales Rooms. Compare the features above, or [contact our team](https://www.shopware.com/en/contact/) for help choosing a plan.

### Is Shopware Community Edition free?

Yes. [Community Edition](https://www.shopware.com/en/community/community-edition/)’s open-source core has no license fee. You arrange hosting, implementation and maintenance, which may involve additional costs. Paid extensions and payment processing may also incur fees. Access to the Shopware Account and Store is subject to our Fair Usage Policy. If the shops linked to your Account generate more than €1 million in gross merchandise value over the preceding 365 days, you need a paid plan to retain access. You can continue using the Community Edition software independently. See our [Fair Usage Policy](https://www.shopware.com/en/news/fair-usage-policy-community-edition/) for details, including GMV reporting requirements. Paid plans also add commercial capabilities and direct support from Shopware.

### How is the price of the Shopware plans calculated?

The pricing for our plans is based on your gross merchandise value (GMV) and further individual factors. Our sales experts will be happy to explain all the details in a personal consultation with you.

### What happens if I cancel?

You can cancel your subscription at any time. Your access will remain active until the end of your current billing cycle, and you will not be charged again after that.

If we ever need to make changes or discontinue the subscription, we aim to give you at least two months’ notice to ensure a guaranteed minimum runtime and a smooth transition.

### When will new agentic features be available in Shopware Intelligence+ for my store?

Agentic capabilities are available now in Shopware Intelligence+. Copilot carries out multi-step tasks across your store, it understands your request, analyses your data and executes. To get started with our agentic capabilities, you must first [sign up to Shopware Intelligence+](https://docs.shopware.com/en/shopware-6-en/shopware-services/shopware-intelligence-plus).

We are committed to continuously evolving Shopware Intelligence+ and aim to expand it regularly with additional services and improvements. You will be informed about new capabilities through ongoing product updates and communications.

### How can I sign up to Shopware Nexus?

If you already have an account, you can get started directly through your Shopware Admin in just a few steps.

1. Install the Nexus service in your Shopware Admin. Go to Settings → Services and activate the Shopware Nexus Event Ingestion Service.
2. Open the [Nexus portal](https://nexus.shopware.com) in your browser.
3. Review and accept the terms and conditions to complete your setup.

Once completed, your store is connected and ready to use Nexus!

### How is Shopware Nexus billed?

Each shop you manage will receive a free monthly execution allowance based on its Shopware plan, with higher-tier plans including larger allowances. Once you exceed your monthly allowance, additional executions are charged in a block of 1,000 at €10 per block. Any additional usage is billed at the end of each month.

### Do I need developer support to use Shopware Nexus?

Today, Shopware Nexus is primarily designed for developers and technical teams, so some technical knowledge may be helpful depending on your use case.

That said, we are continuously improving usability and expanding the interface to make Shopware Nexus more accessible for merchants and operational teams. Our goal is to enable businesses to manage and extend workflows independently, without relying heavily on developer support.

### How will Shopware Nexus help early adopters stay competitive in the Shopware ecosystem?

Nexus helps you deliver projects faster by simplifying integrations and enabling reusable workflows across customers and use cases. Because workflows and connectors remain compatible across Shopware updates, teams spend less time on maintenance and ongoing technical work. This reduces project costs, improves efficiency, and creates more room to offer additional value-added services to customers. Nexus, and the data context of the system landscape it operates within, will allow you to recommend other systems that can help provide more value to your customers - for example, recommending complementary tools or making it easier to migrate from outdated proprietary systems, such as legacy PIM solutions.

### Shopware SaaS, PaaS, or self-hosted? What is the right solution for my business?

Shopware SaaS provides a managed application and hosting environment, including automatic software updates. Shopware PaaS provides managed infrastructure while giving your team greater freedom to customize and develop the application. With self-hosted, you arrange hosting and manage operations yourself or with a partner. With SaaS, hosting and infrastructure maintenance are included in the service. With self-hosted, you budget for hosting and operations separately from any paid Shopware plan. PaaS pricing depends on your infrastructure requirements. Implementation, extensions and other services may add to your total costs.

### I'm currently using a different ecommerce solution. How can I migrate?

Easy - we'll bring you over! Our support team as well as our specialized partner agencies will help you migrate your business smoothly and ensure you keep all your important assets such as customer data, order history, products, etc. [Find further information here.](https://www.shopware.com/en/migration/to-shopware/)

### Can I get any assistance in addition to the official Shopware support?

Of course you can! Because as an open source platform, we involve our global community of business leaders, developers, and partner agencies on a daily basis:

* Chat with over 6,000 users on our [Discord channel](https://chat.shopware.com/)
* Find support and current discussions on the [Stack Overflow](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/tagged/shopware) platform. Use the platform to ask technical questions or share your knowledge
* Gain insights from free training videos
* Join the free Shopware workshops for developers
* Grow and learn with the community on our [Boost Days](https://ecommerce.shopware.com/boost-days)

Please also visit our [documentation](https://docs.shopware.com/en), the [community forum](https://forum.shopware.com/c/shopware-6-english/11), and the [academy](https://www.shopware.com/en/academy/).

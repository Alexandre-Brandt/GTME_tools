## Weflow's Deal Intelligence and Forecasting FAQs

### How does Weflow capture sales activity automatically so reps don't have to update Salesforce manually?

Weflow automatically [captures deal activities like emails and meetings](https://www.weflow.ai/product/activity-capture) directly into Salesforce so your reps can focus on selling. The platform uses AI to auto-update key fields like MEDDIC or next steps and automatically creates buyer contacts. A real-time, two-way sync ensures your CRM records stay current without requiring manual data entry.

### How is Weflow different from Clari and Gong for forecasting and deal intelligence?

Weflow keeps data in your system of record through a real-time, two-way Salesforce sync that automatically maps your fields without manual configuration. You get 50+ AI deal signals and a 0-100 deal health score to spot risks early. Weflow also generates opportunity snapshots that show exactly what changed in your pipeline, when, and why.

### Can Weflow roll up forecasts beyond the standard Salesforce hierarchy?

You can automatically roll up forecast calls across your standard role hierarchy or configure custom team structures in the admin console. Weflow lets you run multiple independent roll-ups for segments like new logo and renewal. Reps submit forecasts on a deal-by-deal basis, giving you complete visibility into the week-on-week changes driving the call.

### How does Weflow help replace spreadsheet-based forecasting with real-time visibility?

Weflow replaces static spreadsheets with 30+ pre-built metrics and dashboards like pacing, waterfall, and stage conversions. You can compare forecasts directly against your pipeline to spot quota gaps early. The platform automatically creates opportunity snapshots to track pipeline changes week-on-week, while AI combines historical and current data to predict a forecast corridor.

### Is Weflow SOC 2 certified and GDPR compliant for handling email and activity data?

Weflow maintains strict security standards, including [SOC 2 Type II](https://trust.weflow.ai/) certification and GDPR compliance. The platform stores captured activity data in native Salesforce objects, honoring your existing permissions, validation rules, and custom objects out-of-the-box. You can review our complete security and compliance details in the Weflow trust center.

### How quickly can we get Weflow running, and will sales reps actually adopt it?

You can get started in minutes using our plug-and-play Salesforce integration that automatically maps fields and respects your custom logic out-of-the-box. Reps adopt the platform quickly because AI automation removes the burden of manual CRM updates. Your team can validate the workflow firsthand through a [guided free pilot](https://www.weflow.ai/demo), rather than a self-serve free trial, before you commit.

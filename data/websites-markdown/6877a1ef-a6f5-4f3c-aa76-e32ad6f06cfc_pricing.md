Usage / features

How does connection pricing work?

Each connection follows a separate cost curve based on usage. As your usage increases, either organically or by adding new tables, your spend rate automatically declines. This means the more you use Fivetran, the more you save.

**Total monthly usage x spend rate = connection monthly cost**

Your **spend rate** (aka unit cost) depends on two things:

1. **Your volume** – The more data you move, the lower your spend rate.
2. **Your pricing plan** – Each pricing plan has its own spend rate.

We apply a $5 base charge to all standard connections that have a monthly usage between 1 MAR and 1M MAR. This *does not* apply to accounts on the Free plan, which remains entirely free.

Read our [pricing guide](/blog/fivetran-pricing-a-simple-guide-with-visuals) to learn more.

What is monthly usage?

Monthly usage refers to the number of [monthly active rows (MAR)](https://fivetran.com/docs/usage-based-pricing#monthlyactiverows) processed by Fivetran in a calendar month. This usage is measured based on per million MAR.

Usage includes:

* **Inserts**: New rows added to your destination.
* **Updates**: Modifications made to existing rows in your destination, including deletes.

Usage excludes:

* **Unchanged rows:** Rows retrieved during scheduled re-syncs that haven't changed since the previous sync.
* **Initial syncs:** The initial bulk data load performed when setting up a connector.

What is a connector?

A connector is a prebuilt data pipeline provided by Fivetran that extracts data from a specific source and loads it to the destination of your choice. These connectors help you establish connections which are individual instances of data pipelines created using Fivetran’s connectors.

What is a connection?

A connection is an individual instance of a data pipeline created using a Fivetran connector to replicate data from a source to a destination.

Once I set up a connection, do I have to move all of my data?

No, you can block schemas or tables from syncing if they don’t contain valuable information. Our pricing model is flexible, so you can add or remove tables or connections at any time. Learn more [here](https://fivetran.com/docs/usage-based-pricing/tracking-and-optimizing-usage#blockschemasortables).

How are database connectors and enterprise database connectors different?

Database connectors include connectors from database sources, with the exception of our enterprise database connectors. Enterprise database connectors include our Oracle database connectors and High-Volume Agent (HVA) connectors. See the full list of our enterprise database connectors [here](https://fivetran.com/docs/usage-based-pricing/billing-and-plans#planlimitations).

Does Fivetran support data residency options including the ability to select regions and specific locations for where my data is processed?

Fivetran runs data connectors on servers in the United States (US), Canada, European Union (EU), United Kingdom (UK), Australia, India, and Singapore. You can select your preferred data processing location when configuring your target destination. All connections configured in a destination run in that destination's designated location. Customers on Fivetran's Enterprise and Business Critical Plans have the added ability to select a cloud provider (AWS or GCP). And, our Business Critical customers have the ability to also select a specific cloud region (ie. us-east-1) to meet stricter data residency requirements.

Activations

How does activations pricing work?

Each activation is priced on MAR and follows a separate cost curve based on usage. As your usage increases, either organically or by adding new activation syncs, your spend rate automatically declines.

What is an activation?

An activation is a set of activations syncs that define how data from various tables and datasets in a specific source are synced to the objects in a specific destination, such as Salesforce or Braze.

Example:  
All syncs from Snowflake → Salesforce A = one activation  
All syncs from Snowflake → HubSpot = another activation  
All syncs from Snowflake → Salesforce B = third activation

What is an activation sync?

An activation sync is an individual sync that moves data from an activation source to an activation destination (distinct from Fivetran connector destinations). Previously, this was called a sync in Census. Multiple activation syncs can write to the same destination.

What is Audience Hub?

Audience Hub is our self-service way to create, test, and optimize audience segments using data in your data warehouse or lake. These segments can then be activated in activation destinations for marketing, customer support, and more.

Transformations

How does transformations pricing work?

We calculate transformations usage based on the total number of successful monthly model runs. Failed runs will not count towards the monthly total. Pricing is usage-based, and the higher the usage, the lower the cost per successful model run. Learn more [here](https://fivetran.com/docs/usage-based-pricing/transformations-pricing).

What are monthly model runs?

Monthly model runs are the number of models successfully executed in a given month. Fivetran uses it as a metric to calculate transformations activity and usage levels.

What is a model run?

A model run is an SQL statement that produces a materialized table or data view. Transformations often consist of multiple interdependent models that work together to produce the final dataset used for business intelligence (BI) or operational purposes.

What happens if I exceed the 5,000 monthly model runs limit on the Free plan?

If you exceed the 5,000 monthly model runs limit on the Free plan, you can either opt to [optimize your usage](https://fivetran.com/docs/usage-based-pricing/transformations-pricing-faq#howcanioptimizemytotalmonthlymodelrunstominimizecosts) or upgrade to a paid plan. The first time you exceed the limit, your transformations will continue to function during the next billing cycle.

If your transformations usage exceeds 5,000 monthly model runs for the second time:

* Fivetran will freeze your connections, transformation jobs, and your account.
* To continue using Fivetran, go to the Plans page in your dashboard and upgrade to a paid plan by the end of the month.

See [Free Plan Terms](/legal/free-plan-terms) for more information.

How can I track my monthly model runs?

The Billing & Usage page in your dashboard provides transformations usage estimates based on historical usage data in your account. There, you can see the usage breakdown by day, the jobs run, and the associated usage for each job.

Free trial

How long can I trial Fivetran?

We offer a risk-free trial experience where you can test our connectors and platform features at no cost for up to 14 days. After you've become a customer, every new connection in your account includes 14 days of free use.

What if I need more time to trial Fivetran?

If you need more time to evaluate or to simply pause the account and timer on your trial during your evaluation, please reach out to your designated product specialist or reach out to us [here,](/contact) and we will be able to help you. You can also add a credit card to keep your data flowing while you explore a full annual contract.

What’s the difference between an account trial and a connector trial?

We offer a [14-day free account trial](https://fivetran.com/docs/getting-started/free-trials#freeaccounttrial), so you can test our [connectors](https://fivetran.com/docs/getting-started/glossary#connector) to see if they meet your business needs. For eligible connections, [Quickstart data models](https://fivetran.com/docs/transformations/data-models) are enabled by default and likewise free for the duration of the trial.

The free trial begins when a connection starts an [incremental sync](https://fivetran.com/docs/getting-started/glossary#incrementalsync) and we detect incremental data. During the account trial period, you can add unlimited connections, review the performance of periodic data updates, analyze your data, and try out other Fivetran features. Learn more in our [new account trial documentation](https://fivetran.com/docs/getting-started/free-trials/account-trial).

Once your 14-day [free account trial](https://fivetran.com/docs/getting-started/free-trials#freeaccounttrial) has ended and your account is active, every connection that you create in your dashboard has 14 days of free use at no cost. A [new connection’s 14-day free use period](https://fivetran.com/docs/getting-started/free-trials#newconnectorfreeuseperiod) begins when our system first detects [usage from an incremental sync](https://fivetran.com/docs/usage-based-pricing#incrementalmar). This free use period automatically applies to all new connections, irrespective of their type. You can have multiple new connections for a connector that are free to use during the period running simultaneously in your account.

Do you offer Enterprise License Agreements (ELAs)?

Fivetran offers Enterprise License Agreements (ELAs), which have a fixed price per year with no limits on consumption, under specific circumstances:

* Customers who intend to move their entire data integration workload onto Fivetran, including workloads that are cost-prohibitive on consumption-based-pricing.
* Customers who prioritize predictability over cost.

For customers who intend to move their entire workload to Fivetran, we price ELAs using a table of target prices based on company size. We adjust these target prices within a band based on objective characteristics of the company and the data workload.

For customers who prioritize predictability, we estimate the cost of their planned workload under consumption based pricing and offer an ELA at a premium to this price. Renewals of these ELAs are priced in the same way.

ELAs do not work for everyone and consumption based pricing is always available, including for customers who have bought ELAs in the past.

Which plan should I choose?

Our [Free Plan](/pricing/free-plan) is great for small teams with low data volumes. Standard is appropriate for organizations with database sources, that need at least 15-minute sync frequencies, or that require access to Fivetran’s REST API. Enterprise is recommended for organizations that require high-volume replication, timely support, and granular roles and permissions. Business Critical is recommended for global enterprise teams who must meet the strictest requirements for data protection and compliance, with features like private networking options and more.

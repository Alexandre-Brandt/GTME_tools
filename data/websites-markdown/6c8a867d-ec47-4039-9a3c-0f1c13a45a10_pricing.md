#### Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the product and billing.

Can I use Aligned for free?

   

## Yes you can! Try out our Free Plan to see the magic in action. No credit card or setup required. No time limited trial. Some of the features are open to Pro and Enterprise Plans only, but our Free Plan includes enough to get you on track to win and onboard customers faster!

   

What is Aligned?

   

Aligned is a collaborative digital workspace between sellers and buyers that helps customer facing teams better engage their customers.

It allows reps to better organize customer resources, conversations, and next steps/mutual plans in a single collaborative space. Reps are empowered to make it easier for customer teams to work with them. They communicate more effectively, better control their deals/accounts, and work smarter with never-before-tracked insights based on their buyers’ digital interactions.

   

Who is Aligned for?

   

To ensure effective use of customer onboarding software, provide proper training for your team members, establish clear communication and collaboration channels, regularly evaluate the onboarding process for improvements, and encourage feedback from both customers and team members to continuously optimize the onboarding experience.

   

Is Aligned for sellers or buyers?

   

Aligned is a tool for customer facing teams that helps both reps and buyers/customers enjoy a simplified and enhanced experience when working together on deals and projects. As a user in Aligned, you can sign up and share engaging workspaces with your prospects and customers. As a buyer, you can access your shared workspace to better collaborate on deals and projects.

Try out our Free Plan (no credit card required).

   

What do buyers/customers think about Aligned?

   

They are huuuuge fans! They truly are.

With so many tools built for improving sales and customer success team productivity, Aligned is the shining light for buyers and customers as it focuses on them, for the first time.

Aligned’s product strategy is ‘buyer-first’. Everything we do, we first ask ourselves “how will our customers’ customers use this?”. The entire platform is built for buyers and customers.

Still unsure? Check out our [G2 Reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/teamaligned/reviews). Many of them talk about how buyers and customers experience Aligned.

   

How secure is my data with Aligned?

   

Very secure. Your data stays private. Top enterprise businesses worldwide choose Aligned every day for a reason.

Super easy! With Aligned, there’s zero setup required. There’s no code required. It’s a simple and intuitive drag & drop environment. Start adding your content, files, mutual action, onboarding & project plans and build personalized workspaces for each prospect or customer within just a few minutes.

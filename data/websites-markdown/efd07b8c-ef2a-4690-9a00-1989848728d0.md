## Questions about Hyperline, answered

Hyperline is the usage-based billing and quote-to-cash platform for AI companies: from the first quote to recognized revenue, with real-time metering, credits and commits, CPQ, invoicing, collections and revenue recognition in one place. [500+ companies](/customers) run billing on it, and Hyperline is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001:2022 certified.

Yes. Hyperline bills the way AI companies sell: tokens, prepaid credits, usage and outcome-based pricing, metered in real time and billed alongside seats and subscriptions. Credits and tokens go [on the quote](/products/cpq), then draw down as customers consume.

Yes. Hyperline meters and prices every event in real time, from the first API call to millions per minute, with no batch jobs. It processes 1B+ events a month with 99.97% uptime, and every billable unit traces back to its raw events. Usage is streamed through the events API or synced from BigQuery, Snowflake and Postgres, as described on the [usage-based billing page](/products/usage-based).

Yes. Prepaid credits and token-based usage run on a [native credit ledger](/products/usage-based) with balance checks, top-up flows, auto-reload and projected-balance webhooks. Contracts carry committed units and minimum amounts, usage is billed against the commitment as events arrive, and overages are rated in real time.

Yes. Hyperline runs the six [quote-to-cash](/products/platform-capabilities/quote-to-cash) steps on one contract: quote, sign, subscribe, invoice, collect and recognize. Invoices include tax in 80+ countries, [collections](/products/accounts-receivable) cover dunning, payment retries and reconciliation, and [revenue recognition](/products/platform-capabilities/revenue-recognition) follows ASC 606 and IFRS 15. Nothing is re-keyed between tools.

Yes. Reps build quotes in Salesforce, HubSpot or Attio and add metered products, prepaid credits and tokens next to seats and flat fees. [Hyperline CPQ](/products/cpq) handles ramps, multi-year terms, multi-step approvals and native e-signature. The signed quote becomes the subscription, with no re-keying into billing.

Hyperline covers the full quote-to-cash cycle, where Stripe Billing focuses on simple subscriptions and Metronome on usage metering. [Stripe Billing](/compare/stripe-billing) works well for simple subscriptions, and Hyperline adds CPQ, usage-based billing, revenue recognition and reporting in one platform. [Metronome](/compare/metronome), now part of Stripe, is usage metering built for engineers, and Hyperline delivers the same real-time usage billing plus CPQ, revenue recognition and a finance-owned UI.

Hyperline puts usage-based billing, CPQ, collections and revenue recognition in one platform, which is the main difference with each of them. [Orb](/compare/orb) does usage-based billing well but does not include CPQ. [Chargebee](/compare/chargebee) covers subscription billing broadly, with usage-based pricing, CPQ and revenue recognition in separate modules. [Lago](/compare/lago) is open source and run by your own team, while Hyperline is a managed platform.

Hyperline carries the same quote through billing, collections and revenue recognition, while DealHub and Salesforce CPQ need separate billing products. [DealHub](/compare/dealhub) does CPQ and contract management well, but it is not a billing platform. [Salesforce CPQ](/compare/salesforce-cpq) quotes inside Salesforce, with billing and revenue recognition sold as separate products. Hyperline also quotes inside Salesforce, through a native widget with two-way sync.

No. Hyperline is not a payment processor: it orchestrates collection through Stripe, Adyen, GoCardless, Mollie and Airwallex, and reconciles the payments it does not process itself. Teams that bill in Stripe can also keep it and add [CPQ for Stripe Billing](/products/cpq/cpq-stripe-billing), with both sides in two-way sync.

Yes. One Hyperline subscription can combine flat fees, seats and metered usage, and invoices show actual usage next to any commitment. [Billing](/products/billing) supports prepaid credits with drawdown, ramps, tiered and volume prices, proration, coupons and wallets. Sales quote the same mix, so the signed quote becomes the hybrid subscription with no re-keying.

Hyperline pricing depends on the plan and on your billing volume, and the team quotes it for you. Plans cover CPQ, billing, usage, collections and reporting, and modules such as accounts receivable and customer intelligence can be added to any plan. Plans and modules are listed on the [pricing page](/pricing).

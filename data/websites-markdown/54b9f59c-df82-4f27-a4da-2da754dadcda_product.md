Confluence knowledge base software keeps content organized, accessible, and easy to manage for internal teams and external customers.

##### Knowledge at your finger tips

Knowledge hoarded is knowledge wasted. Confluence is knowledge base software that gives teams quick and easy access to solutions.

With easy content creation, organization, and a powerful search engine, Confluence provides an accessible platform for knowledge management to help employees and customers help themselves.

---

1. #### Create content with ease

   Collaboratively create and publish knowledge base articles and documentation using pre-made templates. Update easily using a content library & automatic versioning.
2. #### Search less, discover more

   Organize knowledge base articles in a structured hierarchy so it's easy for any user to find. Use a powerful search engine with suggested results to find answers quickly.
3. #### Communicate with customers

   Proactively communicate with your internal and external users with RSS feeds, page watching, and blog updates. Connect with end users via page comments.

## Let Confluence be your single source of truth

Confluence is knowledge base software for today's modern organization, capturing knowledge in a centralized and easy to navigate Space. With a flexible and customizable platform, Confluence helps teams spend less time hunting things down, and more time making things happen.

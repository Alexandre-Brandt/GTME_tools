## Frequently Asked Questions

What Is VoIP?

   

Voice over Internet Protocol (VoIP) is a technology that enables voice communication over the internet instead of traditional phone lines. With VoIP, analog audio signals are converted into digital data packets and transmitted over internet networks. This allows for cost-effective and efficient voice communication, often providing features like video calls, conferencing, and instant messaging.

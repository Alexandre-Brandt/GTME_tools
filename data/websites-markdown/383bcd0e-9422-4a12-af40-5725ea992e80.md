## Frequently asked questions

### What is Tray.ai?

 +  

Tray.ai is the enterprise orchestration platform for data and AI. Build AI agents, govern MCP, connect to 5,000+ apps, databases, APIs, and services, and automate processes — on one platform.

 

### How is Tray.ai different from legacy iPaaS?

 +  

One platform, not a stack of acquired tools. Built AI-native from day one, not retrofitted. Customers ship 3× faster and spend 60% less than with legacy iPaaS (per published Tray.ai customer case studies, 2024–2026). See the full comparisons at /compare.

 

### What is MCP and why does it matter?

 +  

Model Context Protocol (MCP) is the open standard for how AI agents talk to tools and data. Unmanaged MCP carries serious security and cost risks — independent research has reported a 92% exploit rate and that 33% of public MCP servers expose critical vulnerabilities (Backslash Security, 2025). Agent Gateway for MCP is the governance layer that lets enterprises adopt MCP safely.

 

### How quickly can I see results?

 +  

Most customers ship their first agent or integration in under a week. Airbnb turned an 8-week project into one week; Yext shipped 100+ services in 3 months (Tray.ai customer stories).

 

### Is my data secure?

 +  

Tray.ai is SOC 1 and SOC 2 Type 2 audited and HIPAA and GDPR compliant, with audit trails across every agent action, MCP tool call, and workflow run, and 100% execution uptime. Region-specific hosting and on-premise connectivity are available on the Enterprise plan. See the trust report at /trust.

## How to record your screen with Loom?

### Is there a Chrome extension for Loom screen recording?

The Chrome extension for Loom makes it simple to record high-quality video, facecam footage, and audio right from your browser.

1. **Install the Loom Chrome extension [here](/download)**. Loom for Chrome offers basic features. For advanced tools such as drawing, virtual backgrounds, or HD recording, download the Loom [desktop app](/desktop).
2. **Choose what to capture**. Select **Screen Only** to record just your screen. To record your screen and webcam simultaneously, select **Screen and Camera.**
3. Once you have the desired settings, click **Start Recording**. A three-second countdown timer will appear, then your recording will begin.
4. **Stop recording**. When you’ve captured everything you need, click the square red record button on the menu to bring your recording to an end.
5. **Edit and share.** After recording, Loom automatically copies the video link to your clipboard. Your video will open in your browser where you can view, edit, and share. Learn more about [recording with the Loom Chrome extension.](https://support.loom.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002187698-How-to-get-started-with-the-Loom-Chrome-extension)

### Can you screen record on an Android using Loom?

1. [Signup for Loom](https://www.loom.com/signup).
2. Download the[Loom Android App](https://www.loom.com/mobile).
3. Record your front-facing or back-facing camera, android screen, or both.
4. Once finished, Loom instantly generates a link for you to share. No more uploading or waiting for a video to render!

### How can I record my iPhone screen on Loom?

1. [Signup for Loom](https://www.loom.com/signup).
2. Download the [Loom iOS App](https://www.loom.com/mobile).
3. Record your entire iPhone or iPad screen, or record with your front-facing or back-facing camera.
4. Once finished, Loom instantly generates a link for you to share. No more uploading or waiting for a video to render!

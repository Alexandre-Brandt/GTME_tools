## Agentsget lost in your complex systems.

Start wherever it hurts most.

Can't connect what you already know.

Can't remember what you just did.

Can't follow your rules.

[01For teamsCONNECTED

### Knowledge is scattered

The answer is spread across a doc, a ticket, and your Claude session, leaving your colleagues to redo work already done.

Cognee connects all your data into one brain.Learn more →](/company-brain)[02For agent buildersREMEMBERED

### Agent experience is discarded

Your agent is solving a task it already solved last week, wasting tokens and your time.

Cognee gives your agents memory.Learn more →](/product/cognee-sdk)[03For enterprisesFOLLOWED

Your organization runs on rules no model was trained on, so your agents invent their own.

Cognee generates the ontologies your agents follow.Learn more →](/product/enterprise#auto-ontologies)

How we build

## Your company builds a shared company brain for agents.

[5 minLocal

### Give your agent durable memory

Install Cognee and connect it to Claude Code, Cursor, or any MCP client. Your agent keeps project context across sessions. No new infrastructure.

$ pip install cognee

Solo devs · Agent hackersRun the quickstart](https://docs.cognee.ai/quickstart)[1 dayConnect

### Connect the data your agents should know

We connect your GitHub org, Slack, or Linear — plus warehouses, docs, and APIs — into one recallable memory layer. Add a source once, and every agent can search it.

$ cognee.add("github://your-org")

Data + platform teamsSee connectors](https://docs.cognee.ai/integrations)[1 weekProduction

### Ship agents that understand your domain

We deploy Cognee BYOC — in your own cloud — behind customer-facing agents, so they retrieve cited facts, follow domain rules, and improve from real usage.

$ cognee.search("…") # cited answers

Features

Enriched profiles

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6194c0787e082a13b6aa29b4/63ecd2c766c3ca346a6da968_chevron-down.svg)

Access enriched profiles including email addresses, skills, interests, work experience, and social links.

Intro requests

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6194c0787e082a13b6aa29b4/63ecd2c766c3ca346a6da968_chevron-down.svg)

Send email intro requests to get in touch with the connections of other members on your Swarm.

Connection strength

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6194c0787e082a13b6aa29b4/63ecd2c766c3ca346a6da968_chevron-down.svg)

See the relationship strength between your members and their connections.

Company-level filters

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6194c0787e082a13b6aa29b4/63ecd2c766c3ca346a6da968_chevron-down.svg)

Search companies by size and location and find out who can make warm intros.

Company and profile tags

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6194c0787e082a13b6aa29b4/63ecd2c766c3ca346a6da968_chevron-down.svg)

Assign tags to companies or profiles and easily organize your network.

Saved lists

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6194c0787e082a13b6aa29b4/63ecd2c766c3ca346a6da968_chevron-down.svg)

Build and manage lists of potential candidates, sales leads, and more, so you and your members can add relevant, warm connections. Lists are viewable and editable across your team by default, but they can also be made private.

Google Calendar

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6194c0787e082a13b6aa29b4/63ecd2c766c3ca346a6da968_chevron-down.svg)

Import connections from your primary Google Calendar (people you've met with).

AI Network Mapper

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6194c0787e082a13b6aa29b4/63ecd2c766c3ca346a6da968_chevron-down.svg)

See the work overlaps of colleagues or stakeholders without having them create an account.

HubSpot integration

Pull your relationship data in your HubSpot instance.

Clay.com integration

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6194c0787e082a13b6aa29b4/63ecd2c766c3ca346a6da968_chevron-down.svg)

API & MCP Access

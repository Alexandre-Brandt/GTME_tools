## Record at scale without slowing down for legal

GDPR compliant from day one, built in Germany for the DACH region and the EU — the highest privacy standards on the market. The compliance guardrails are already in the product, so rollout for works councils and DPOs takes days, not quarters.

Consent and permissions.
:   Two-step consent with a notetaker announcement in every call, and group-based recording permissions your admins control.

Retention and access control.
:   Configurable retention policies, role-based access, and full audit logging — certified to ISO 27001:2022.

EU data residency.
:   Your data stays on EU servers, never trains AI models, and never crosses the Atlantic.

Anonymization for AI.
:   Automatically strip names, companies and personal data before any transcript reaches an LLM — AI insights without exposing customer data, which most tools simply can’t offer.

Frequently asked questions

### What is pricing based on?

What is pricing based on?

Our pricing follows a credit-based system. The Free plan includes 50 company credits and 200 API credits per month. For higher usage needs, paid plans begin at $49 monthly, with volume discounts available on larger plans.

### How does a company credit work?

How does a company credit work?

A company credit allows you to access to all job postings, technologies used and firmographics (location, industry, size, etc.) of a company through app.theirstack.com.

### How does an API credit work?

How does an API credit work?

API credits are used for API requests or webhooks. API credits are consumed for each record (job or company) returned from our API endpoints or dispatched via Webhooks.

### Can I use TheirStack just for exporting data once?

Can I use TheirStack just for exporting data once?

Yes, you can. You have the option to purchase a credits package (one-time payment) or subscribe for just one month and cancel once you've finished your work.

### Can I cancel my TheirStack subscription at any time?

Can I cancel my TheirStack subscription at any time?

Yes, you can. You can modify or cancel your plan anytime from your dashboard. Even after canceling, you can access your paid plan until your current subscription period ends.

### When do credits reset? Do they rollover?

When do credits reset? Do they rollover?

For monthly subscription, you will receive new credits each month on your billing date.

For yearly subscription, you receive all credits upfront, which reset annually on your subscription anniversary.

Unused paid credits will roll over and remain in your account for 12 months after they're purchased.

### Can I share my subscription with my team?

Can I share my subscription with my team?

Yes, you can. You can invite your team members to join your workspace and share the credits with them. There is no limit to the number of users you can invite.

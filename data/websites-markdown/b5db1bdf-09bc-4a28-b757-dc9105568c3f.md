## Most vendors ship stale, wrong or disconnected numbers.

That means fewer conversations, less efficient reps and wasted data spend.

In Clay's 2026 test of mobile data providers, Forager ranked #1 of 10 by quality score, at an optimal price per valid number.

What “right person” means

1. **Does it connect?** A number that is dead or disconnected wastes the call.
2. **Does the name match?** A live number for someone else is worse than none.
3. **Is it the profile you asked about?** Not a namesake, not a former owner of the number.

FAQ

## Questions buyers ask first

### What data does Forager provide?

⌄

Mobile numbers, personal and work emails, person and company enrichment, people search and job postings, for 800M+ people and 65M+ companies, by API or data feed, or in the Forager web app.

### Am I charged when you find nothing?

⌄

No. You pay for numbers we find, not lookups that miss. A mobile or email lookup that returns nothing costs 0 credits.

### Why are Forager's mobiles more expensive?

⌄

Every mobile is validated for connectivity, name match and profile match before it reaches you. If a number turns out to be wrong, it is credited back through the bad-data endpoint.

### Is Forager GDPR compliant?

⌄

Forager is GDPR-aligned. No Article 9 sensitive data is collected, access and erasure requests are honoured within 30 days, and a DPA is available on every contract. [Read the GDPR page](/gdpr-compliance)

### Can I test before I commit?

⌄

Yes. Create a free account and run your own sample, or book a demo and send us a list: we run it and walk you through what we found.

### Can I show Forager data inside my own product?

⌄

Yes. Display and resale terms are available for platforms; book a demo to discuss volume and rights.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can multiple teams use Flowla?

![Plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/638e09f4e83fdd19c81b7cc8/638e09f4e83fdd7a301b7cf5_icon_plus.svg)

Yes. Unlike most digital sales rooms that focus purely on pre-signature deals, Flowla supports the full end-to-end journey, where CS and RevOps are never an afterthought.  
  
In fact, our happiest customers are those that use Flowla across multiple teams. That's when you see the full impact:  
  
- Your customers experience one consistent, continuous, well-supported journey  
- Handoffs between teams feel fluid, instead of disjointed across tools and processes  
- Sales, CS, and RevOps share visibility into engagement and progress, all aligned around one source of truth  
  
If you have specific questions, you can book a short call with us.

### Can Flowla integrate with my existing tools?

![Plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/638e09f4e83fdd19c81b7cc8/638e09f4e83fdd7a301b7cf5_icon_plus.svg)

Yes. Flowla offers native integrations with CRMs like Salesforce, HubSpot, Attio, and others.  
  
It also connects with tools like Slack, Gong, Fireflies, Gmail, Outlook, and more, so you can build end-to-end workflows without disrupting your current processes.

### How does implementation work?

![Plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/638e09f4e83fdd19c81b7cc8/638e09f4e83fdd7a301b7cf5_icon_plus.svg)

Flowla's onboarding and implementation process is designed to be very easy and straightforward, so you can be up and running in 14 days or less.  
  
Our support team is also available to help you every step of the way, with personalized onboarding, training, and ongoing support to ensure a smooth transition.

### How does Flowla accommodate enterprise-level needs?

![Plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/638e09f4e83fdd19c81b7cc8/638e09f4e83fdd7a301b7cf5_icon_plus.svg)

Flowla is built with scalability in mind and offers flexible options for larger organizations. It can:  
  
- Handle complex workflows  
- Support multiple teams and users  
- Integrate with your existing enterprise systems to ensure a smooth experience  
  
If your organization has specific requirements, get in touch and our product experts will be happy to explore how they can support you.

### Is Flowla safe?

![Plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/638e09f4e83fdd19c81b7cc8/638e09f4e83fdd7a301b7cf5_icon_plus.svg)

Yes. Flowla was built with both your and your customers' privacy in mind. It follows strict data protection standards and is compliant with SOC2 Type II, GDPR (Europe and UK), PECR, and CCPA.  
  
You can access our Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Data Processing Agreement, and data compliance memo on our Security page.

### How many rooms can I create?

![Plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/638e09f4e83fdd19c81b7cc8/638e09f4e83fdd7a301b7cf5_icon_plus.svg)

On Starter, you get up to 20 rooms to try Flowla with your clients and see the impact for yourself, no credit card required. On Team and Enterprise, rooms are unlimited, so your whole team can create as many as they need.

### Do you offer a free trial?

![Plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/638e09f4e83fdd19c81b7cc8/638e09f4e83fdd7a301b7cf5_icon_plus.svg)

Yes. With Flowla's free Starter plan, you can create up to 20 rooms to try Flowla with your clients and see the impact for yourself. Get started by booking a short 15-minute call with us.

### What's a credit, and will 500 be enough?

![Plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/638e09f4e83fdd19c81b7cc8/638e09f4e83fdd7a301b7cf5_icon_plus.svg)

Credits cover REX's AI work: room generation, document and asset creation, signals, and the rest of the suite. 500 credits a seat each month comfortably covers a very active AE running around 20 live deals, with a weekly meeting per deal and AI docs in every room. Heavier usage is handled on Enterprise.

### What happens if I need more AI usage?

![Plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/638e09f4e83fdd19c81b7cc8/638e09f4e83fdd7a301b7cf5_icon_plus.svg)

AI usage above the monthly allowance is covered on the Enterprise plan, with custom credits sized to your team. Reach out and we'll work out the right level for you.

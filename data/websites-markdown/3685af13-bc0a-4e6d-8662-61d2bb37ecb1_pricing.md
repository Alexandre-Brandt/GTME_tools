## How much does AskElephant cost?

AskElephant Core is $99 per user/month when billed annually, and White-Glove is $119 per user/month when billed annually. Enterprise pricing is custom. Plans include platform access, revenue work capacity, integrations, and different levels of premium service support.

## What is included in AskElephant pricing?

All AskElephant plans include platform access service. Core includes CRM auto-update, unlimited premium automations, premium integrations, MCP / API access, universal AI notetaking, unlimited data storage, dedicated onboarding, and 24/7 support. White-Glove adds dedicated CSM coverage, premium services / Forward Deployed Engineers, priority support, and a dedicated Slack channel. Enterprise is shaped around custom CRM automation, integrations, data migration, custom MCP support, and SOC 2 / HIPAA needs.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is CPQ (Configure, Price, Quote)?

CPQ is a sales tool for companies to quickly and accurately generate quotes for orders. CPQ applications often work in tandem with CRM platforms, ERP programs, and other business technology, which helps ensure integrated data and accuracy.

### What is a CPQ solution?

[CPQ stands for Configure, Price, Quote](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/cpq/what-is-cpq/). A CPQ solution is a tool that helps businesses automate the configuration, pricing, and quoting of their products and services. This makes the sales process more efficient, improves compliance, and accelerates deal cycles. [Learn about the benefits of cloud CPQ software](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/cpq/software/cloud/).

### What is the difference between CRM and CPQ?

[CRM](https://www.salesforce.com/crm/what-is-crm/) stands for Customer Relationship Management, a software solution that manages every touchpoint in the sales process, from early prospecting to deal close. CPQ stands for Configure, Price, Quote. CPQ solutions help teams manage the latter part of a sales process before a contract is signed. [Salesforce CPQ](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/cpq/what-is-salesforce-cpq/) is one example of a CPQ solution, helping businesses automate the configuration, pricing, and quoting of products and services.

### How long does it take to implement Salesforce CPQ?

Timelines vary based on product complexity, the number of pricing rules, and integration requirements. Simple deployments can go live in weeks; enterprise-scale implementations with complex configuration rules typically take 3–6 months. Salesforce partners specialize in Revenue Cloud deployments and can accelerate time-to-value with AI-assisted tooling.

### Why choose Salesforce for CPQ?

Revenue Cloud is built natively on Salesforce — your product catalog, pricing rules, CRM data, approval workflows, and revenue reporting all live in one platform. Agentforce brings agentic AI to every step of the quote-to-cash process, and a global partner ecosystem supports every deployment at any scale.

Agentforce brings agentic AI to every step of configure, price, and quote. ### What is revenue management?

[Revenue management](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/revenue-lifecycle-management/what-is-revenue-management/) is the process of optimizing every business activity in the [quote-to-cash](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/cpq/quote-to-cash/) cycle for speed and accuracy. This includes everything from setting pricing and packaging to quote configuration, contract drafting, order fulfillment, [invoicing](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/cpq/sales-invoice/), billing, and reporting. The most efficient revenue management processes include automation and AI that streamline time-consuming manual work.

### What is a revenue management platform?

A revenue management platform is designed to help business manage their entire revenue lifecycle — from setting prices and purchasing channels, to tracking sales, generating quotes, and managing billing. Effective revenue management platforms streamline revenue tracking while maximizing revenue growth via the right channels and strategies.

### Benefits of revenue management platforms

Revenue management platforms streamline order, quoting, and billing processes; allow companies to set and manage complex pricing structures; gauge customer demand; and when powered by AI and automation, handle tedious manual work and data analysis that frees sales teams and business leaders to focus on strategic work. The best platforms also ensure that data collection and analysis happens in real-time, giving leaders the information they need to maximize revenue.

### What are the main features of the top revenue management platforms?

Revenue management platforms generally offer tools for setting and tracking prices and pricing models, managing orders and product inventory, generating quotes for deals, billing, and revenue reporting/forecasting. More advanced platforms also use AI and automation to handle repetitive, manual, or time-consuming tasks, like data analysis, that can give business leaders real-time guidance on strategic decisions.

### What industries use revenue management platforms?

Revenue management platforms are used in any industry that tracks revenue — all of them, basically. They are particularly common in industries where physical goods and/or variable demand are the centerpiece of business. This includes manufacturing, hospitality, travel, telecom, and media.

### How can sales automation & AI improve revenue management?

[Sales automation](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/what-is-sales-automation/) and [sales AI](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/ai/) can improve revenue management by automating manual tasks for better efficiency. They also optimize elements of the sales, fulfillment, and billing process to ensure deal accuracy, customer satisfaction, and on-time payments.

### How can sales forecasting help improve revenue management?

[Sales forecasting](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/analytics/sales-forecasting-guide/) helps improve revenue management by providing accurate sales projections leaders can use to adjust strategy, allocate resources, and update business plans to ensure growth.

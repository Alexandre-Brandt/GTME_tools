# Pricing & plans

ZoomInfo offers custom packages based on your B2B data use cases and needs. Available via our GTM platform or entirely headless via [GTM.ai](https://www.gtm.ai).

![](/_next/static/media/ranking-ribbon.3l4kb6g5ohmic.svg)

## Tell us the job. We scope the data, the agents, and the governance to it.

Every use case runs on the same verified record and the same platform. What changes is which data you pull, how much of it, and what governance you need around it.

### Prospecting & pipeline

Find buyers, run sequences, build pipeline.

### Account-based marketing

Identify, reach, and convert target accounts, down to the visitor on your site.

### Lead management

Score, route, and convert inbound leads while the intent is still live.

### Deal execution

Map the buying committee and act on the signals that keep deals moving.

### Data enrichment

One always-fresh source of truth feeding every system you run on.

### Customer expansion

See renewal risk and room to grow before the QBR, not after it.

### Revenue intelligence

Understand why deals are won and lost, and coach reps from their own calls.

### Talent acquisition

Find the best talent, faster

### Something else?

Describe the job in your own words and we'll tell you what it takes.

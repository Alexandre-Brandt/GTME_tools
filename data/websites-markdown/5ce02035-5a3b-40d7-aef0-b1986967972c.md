## Meet your agents.

Scout finds the right number. Operator gets a live person on the line. Coach helps reps prepare and improve on every call. Three agents, one system, every rep performing at their best.

## Scout: Your agents for better phone data

The prospects are already on your list. The problem is the numbers. Scout pulls your dialing history, flags the dead ends, and finds the number that answers.

[explore scout](/platform/scout)

## Operator: Your agents for faster connections

Parallel dialing shouldn't cost you the opening moment of a conversation. Operator doesn't just skip the screeners and phone trees, it handles them: drops the voicemail, clears the tree, and puts your rep on only when someone's actually there.

[explore operator](/platform/dialer)

## Coach: Your agents for sharper conversations

Coach listens to every call, so managers don’t have to. It listens for the objection, finding the battlecard that answers it, scores every interaction, and runs practice sessions on demand.

[Explore Coach](/platform/coaching-and-enablement)

## Need To Know More?

Orum combines AI-powered dialing, connect rate intelligence, and an AI coaching suite so reps spend more time talking to buyers and managers know who and what to coach to increase conversion. The result: more live conversations per hour, integrated coaching, and more meetings.

SEPs and CRMs manage sequences and digital outreach; Orum is purpose-built to maximize live conversations. We sit alongside your existing tools, optimizing connect rate, dialing strategy, and coaching so those systems see more high-quality activity and pipeline.

If two people pick up, we detect who answered first down to the millisecond, automatically connect you to that person, and end/cancel the other call — they just see a missed call, and that number is dropped into your Canceled Calls bucket so you can easily call them back afterward.

Orum integrates with leading CRMs (like Salesforce, HubSpot) and SEPs (like Outreach, Salesloft, Gong Engage, Apollo), along with Webhooks for custom workflows. We plug into your existing stack so calls, outcomes, and recordings flow where your team already works.

We offer core plans like Launch and Ascend so you can match Orum to your team’s stage and calling volume, plus add-ons for things like Webhooks and AI Coaching. Pricing is per user, billed annually, with options for lighter-use roles and enterprise needs.

Most teams are live in days, not weeks. Our onboarding team handles setup, integrations, and rep training, and you’ll have access to ongoing CSM support, live chat, and a full help center to keep performance trending up over time.

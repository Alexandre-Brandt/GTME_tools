Can I search without a professional profile URL?

Yes. You can find a contact using just their full name and company name. This is unique to Datagma and especially useful when enriching lists where professional profiles aren't available.

Datagma is a native provider in Clay. Add your API key and place it wherever it fits your workflow, as your first enrichment step or alongside other providers. It only charges on successful matches, so you maximize coverage while minimizing cost.

Yes. Datagma supports BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) on Fullenrich, BetterContact, and GetCargo. Add your API key in their interface and Datagma runs as part of their enrichment waterfall automatically.

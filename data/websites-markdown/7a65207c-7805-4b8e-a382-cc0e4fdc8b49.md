## It's like having radar for your social signals

### We monitor your team's profile visits, connections, posts, likes, comments, shares—even your company profile and many more assets for social engagement. Then we tell you who to reach out to. You decide how.

#### Collect, qualify, enrich and act

Whether someone visits your profile or that of a colleague, likes your post or follows your company page -- whatever happens on your social assets, we collect that signal.

Then AI checks if that person matches your ICP criteria and enriches it with email, phone, and other verified attributes.

And off it goes to whichever tool you use in your stack or playbook.

[More about data enrichment](/sales-data-enrichment)

## Identify 100% of the people who engage with your team, long before they ever reach your website

### Website visitor identification only uncovers around 30% of your traffic. It is often inaccurate, and it arrives very late in the buying process. We give you every person who engaged with you, your team or your content, by name, long before they visit your website.

### Every engagement becomes a signal

We catch profile visits on your teammates' profiles and your company page, plus likes, comments, shares, follows and new connections. Also tracked competitor posts, influencer activity, and any keyword you want watched.

Nothing expires after three days. Nothing gets missed because somebody was on holiday.

## Frequently Asked Questions

How do I define my ICP?  + −  

Inside the platform, you set persona criteria (job titles, seniority, roles) and company criteria (industry, size, location). The AI qualification engine uses those rules to score every incoming signal automatically.

  How long does setup take?  + −  

Most teams are up and running within a day. You connect your accounts, set your ICP criteria, and signals start flowing. No engineering required.

  What can workflows do?  + −  

Workflows are "if this, then that" automations. A signal comes in, meets a condition, triggers an action — send a Slack alert, create a HubSpot contact, fire a webhook to Clay, send a connection request. You set the rules; the platform executes them.

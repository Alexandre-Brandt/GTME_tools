Primeforge is a cold email infrastructure tool that allows you to set up domains and mailboxes specifically for cold outreach at scale. Those are specifically Google Workspace and MS365 mailboxes. As most prospects use those two providers for their emails, this means you can increase your email deliverability by sending from the same providers.

Primeforge, Mailforge, and Infraforge each serve different cold email infrastructure needs. Primeforge provides high-quality Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 mailboxes, enabling users to send emails from the same providers most prospects use - maximizing deliverability and inbox placement at scale. Mailforge offers shared cold email infrastructure with automated DNS setup, making it a fast and cost-effective solution for getting started, though with limited control. Infraforge, on the other hand, delivers fully private infrastructure with advanced features such as multi-IP provisioning, API and whitelabel options, ideal for teams or agencies requiring maximum scalability, control, and branding flexibility.

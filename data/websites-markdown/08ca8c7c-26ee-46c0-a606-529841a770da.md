## No other tool runs your whole setup by voice.

No single vendor unifies your agents: Anthropic won't run your Codex work, OpenAI won't show your Claude threads. yothere holds all three, the open-source OpenClaw included, and lets you hail and run up to five at once by voice, hands-free.

### Just say what you need

Say it out loud on a live voice call. You can also type it in the cockpit or start it from the CLI or your editor. One line starts a session.

### Up to five run at once

Sessions run on your own machine, on the Claude or ChatGPT subscription you already pay for. Up to five advance at the same time while your machine is awake. Nothing goes out (no code pushed, no email sent) until you approve it, and cost caps stay on by default.

### Only the real decision reaches you

Most sessions finish on their own, so your inbox usually stays empty. When one needs you, a push lands on your phone and taps straight through to that single decision, answerable by voice or one tap from anywhere.

## Read the diff, then ship it.

A code thread works on a branch in its own git worktree and hands you back the diff to read. Press Ship it and yothere pushes the branch and opens the PR as you. No approval can hand an agent a push: you publish, or nobody does.

## Talk to five sessions. They talk back.

You are in the car. Talk to your sessions hands-free on a live voice call, start the next one, and hear where each stands. When a thread needs you, a push lands and taps straight through to the one decision. Voice is hosted: no phone number, no keys, nothing to set up. 120 minutes a day, reset every morning.

## Real work, on your terms

Dispatch a task and let it run on your own machine. yothere keeps it yours: outward sends are held for your approval, and it reaches you on any device.

### Hands-free voice

Say what you need on a live call and hear your sessions answer back. Start work, check progress, or approve a decision without touching your machine. Voice is hosted: no phone number, no keys, nothing to set up.

### Runs on your machine

Your files, your subscription, your fleet. yothere never holds your model credentials and never bills you for inference.

### Safe by default

Cost caps per thread and per day. A send-gate holds every outward action until you approve it.

### Permission tiers per machine

Read-only, workspace, or full. You pick each machine's tier in the browser when you approve it, and you can revoke a device from anywhere.

### Pairing without pasted secrets

yothere login prints a short code. You approve the machine in your own logged-in browser, and no token is ever pasted to an agent.

### Asks only when it must

Real decisions surface. Everything else ships without a status meeting.

### Answer from your phone

Install the cockpit on your phone and turn the bell on. A push deep-links to the one blocked thread, and a quick reply keeps the whole fleet moving.

### Bring your own agent

Claude Code and Codex on the subscriptions you already pay for, plus the open-source OpenClaw. The cockpit is ours; the agent stays yours. OpenClaw runs contained: read-only or workspace tier, never full.

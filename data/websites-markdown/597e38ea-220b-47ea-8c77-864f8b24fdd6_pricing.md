### How credits work

Credits are usage units consumed when data enrichment or activation actions are performed across the platform. [Learn more about credits](#faq-credits).

### What counts as an identified company

A unique company identified from your website traffic within the month. Multiple visits from the same company count as one identified company.

### How is the price calculated?

Your price tier is based on how many companies we identify monthly. If the same company visits your site multiple times per month they still count as one. We filter out ISPs and low-quality traffic.

## Pricing, answered.

What are credits?

Credits are usage units consumed when you enrich data or trigger activation actions — like pushing a company to your CRM, enriching a contact's email or phone, or running a Campaign. Identifying companies visiting your website doesn't use credits; that's included in your plan's company limit.

How do I know how many credits I need?

It depends on how actively your team enriches data and triggers automations each month. As a starting point: lower-volume teams validating the product fit well on Discover, while teams running regular outbound, ABM, or CRM sync workflows typically need Activate or Scale. [Talk to us and we'll size it in 10 minutes.](/request-demo/?subject=lf-pricing)

What counts as an identified company?

A unique company resolved from your website traffic within a given month. If the same company visits your site multiple times in a month, it still counts as one identified company — only the first identification counts against your plan limit.

One exception: if a company has employees working from multiple office locations, each location may appear separately. That's intentional — it tells you which office is showing interest, so you know exactly who to reach out to.

[Learn more in our Help Center.](https://help.leadfeeder.com/en/articles/13922369-what-is-considered-an-identified-company)

Is Web Insights included in my plan?

Yes. Every plan includes the company-level reports, with three months of history on Lite and two years from Discover up. The AI summary and Ask a question start on Discover.

What's the difference between the plans?

The three paid plans share the same core identification capability. Where they differ is credit volume, company limit, and which features are unlocked. Campaigns and CRM automations require Activate or above. A dedicated CSM is included on Scale. **Not sure which fits?** [**Book a quick call.**](/request-demo/)

For the Platform plan most teams are up and running within a few days. Once we understand your goals, we'll tailor the setup and provide hands-on support to get you seeing value fast.

Does my whole team need separate seats?

No. Every plan — including Lite — includes unlimited users. The whole team shares one credit pool, so there's no reason to limit who has access.

What happens when I run out of credits?

You can top up with extra credits at any time in blocks of 100 to 5,000. These are non-renewing and useful for seasonal campaigns or one-off projects. You can also upgrade your plan for a higher monthly credit allowance.

Do unused credits roll over?

Extra (one-off) credits are valid for two years. Standard plan credits follow your billing cadence — annual plans receive a lump sum at the start of the year, monthly plans receive credits each month. Standard credits don't roll over at the end of the contract period.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Lite gives you access to the platform with the last 100 identified companies and a 7-day history, with unlimited users. It's a good way to see how visitor identification works before committing to a paid plan.

How does annual billing work?

Annual plans are available at a significant discount versus monthly. Discover and Activate support annual paid upfront or monthly. Scale supports annual paid upfront or quarterly. [Talk to our team to know more.](/request-demo/)

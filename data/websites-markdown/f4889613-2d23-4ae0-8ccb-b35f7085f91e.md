## Frequently asked questions

Can't find the answer you're looking for? Email us at [support@gamma.app](mailto:support@gamma.app).

Can I use Gamma for free?

Yes. Gamma offers a free plan with AI credits to get started. Paid plans include additional credits and capabilities for individuals and teams. Visit our [pricing page](https://gamma.app/pricing) to compare plans.

What can I create with Gamma?

Create presentations, websites, documents, graphics, and social content. Start with a prompt, an outline, or a file, then work with Gamma's AI agent to develop your story and design. Refine the result with AI or edit it yourself.

How can I create beautifully designed, on-brand presentations with AI?

Describe the look you want or share visual inspiration, and Gamma helps shape a design around your content. Use brand controls, workspace templates, and custom instructions to keep work consistent. Refine layouts with AI or arrange content yourself with freeform mode to make the presentation feel like yours. Feature availability [varies by plan](https://gamma.app/pricing).

Can I import and export PowerPoint presentations with Gamma?

Yes. Bring an existing PowerPoint or PDF into Gamma, then refine the content and design with AI or by hand. Improved import and export fidelity helps preserve your formatting as you move between tools. Export to PowerPoint to continue working there, or download a PDF to share.

Can I use Gamma with my team?

Yes. Create a shared workspace and give your team a consistent starting point with workspace templates, brand controls, and custom instructions. Enterprise capabilities include SSO, SCIM, permissions, and admin controls. Feature availability [varies by plan](https://gamma.app/pricing).

How do sharing and collaboration work?

Your projects are private by default. Choose whether to share with specific people, your team, or publicly, and collaborate on the same project in real time. You control who can access each project.

Can I connect my other tools and AI agents to Gamma?

Yes. Bring content from tools like Slack, Notion, and Salesforce into Gamma, or create presentations from AI assistants like [ChatGPT](https://gamma.app/integrations/chatgpt) and [Claude](https://gamma.app/integrations/claude). Build on the context you already have and turn it into work you can refine and share, with less copying and pasting. [See all integrations](https://gamma.app/integrations).

Does Gamma train on my data?

For [Business and Enterprise](https://gamma.app/pricing) workspaces, content is automatically excluded from AI training. For individual Free, Plus, Pro, and Ultra workspaces, content may be used to train AI features by default, but you can opt out in your account settings.

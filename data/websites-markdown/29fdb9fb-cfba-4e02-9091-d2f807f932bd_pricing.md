Every risk surfaced, brief generated, or question answered draws from your pre-packaged consumption. Anyone in the org can ask, seat or no seat.  
   
  
Usage means your team walks in informed instead of blind.

![AI Integrations](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/69aab19e3344fee750b8721c/6a56a408769ce24d85ad6807_31.svg)

##### True Forward pricing

Most contracts true up: they look back and bill you for what you overused. True Forward looks ahead instead. Use more than your pre-packaged consumption? Nothing shuts off and nothing gets back-billed.  
  
At renewal, you adjust forward to what you actually used - never back-billed for the term you're in, only sized correctly for the next one.

## Questions, answered.

### Why usage-based instead of per-seat?

Answers only pay off when people use them. So we open access to the whole go-to-market team, not just the people with budget for a login, and you pay for what gets used.

### What counts as an answer?

Reading a dashboard is free. You pay when Backstory delivers something your team uses: a risk surfaced, a deal summary, a coaching flag, a question answered.

### Is my spend predictable?

Yes. Your consumption is pre-packaged at the start of the term, so you know the number before anyone uses a thing. Nothing meters against you mid-term and nothing gets back-billed, so the figure you sign is the figure you pay for that term. If usage runs ahead, you don't get a surprise invoice - you adjust forward at renewal to match what your team actually used. Budget once, no mid-year true-ups.

### Who gets charged?

The people whose customer activity powers the platform carry the platform fee. Anyone who pulls an answer draws from your pre-packaged consumption, whatever their role, so seats stay free.

### What's True Forward, and how does it save us money?

In your first term, consumption comes pre-packaged, sized as a starting estimate. If you use more, nothing shuts off and you're not back-billed. At renewal, your term adjusts forward to what you actually used. Because the initial package is set before anyone's used a thing, most teams start out paying less than a per-seat contract would have cost.

### What if we use more than we packaged?

Good sign. More usage means your team is pulling more answers and getting more out of the platform. Nothing stops mid-term. At renewal you size forward to that real usage and keep paying only for what proves valuable.

### What if we grow fast?

Connect more customer-facing people and your platform fee rises by those people, not by everyone watching. New managers, a bigger ops team, still free.

Pricing FAQ

Why is Datagma more affordable than enterprise solutions?

We focus on data quality and API efficiency, not expensive enterprise sales teams. You get premium EMEA/APAC coverage at a fair price, starting at $49/mo.

What's included in all plans?

All plans include: API access, WhatsApp integration, CatchALL emails free, Chrome extension, and file upload up to 50k rows.

How do credits work?

Credits are one pool you spend on emails or phones. 1 credit = 1 verified email, 30 credits = 1 mobile phone. So 1,000 credits = 1,000 emails, OR 33 phones, or a mix (for example 500 emails and 16 phones). You only spend credits when we find verified data.

Do unused credits expire?

No. Unused credits roll over for up to 12 months on paid plans.

Is there a contract or minimum commitment?

No. Month-to-month billing. Cancel anytime. Annual billing gets you 20% off but isn't required.

Can my team share credits?

Yes. Credits are per account, not per seat. Your whole team shares the same credit pool — however many seats your plan includes (Free/Regular: 1, Popular: 3, Expert: 5), plus any extra seats you add at $5/user/mo.

Do you charge for empty results?

No. You only pay when we find a verified email or phone. No match = no credit used.

## Frequently asked questions

We recommend Premium for new AI users, Super for daily agent users, and Ultra for power users. Plus is recommended for social creators with limited AI usage.

The Teams plan is recommended for professionals collaborating with others, and larger organizations that need invoicing, pooled usage, or advanced security should choose Enterprise.

Self-serve plans support all major credit and debit cards. For invoice-based billing and wire transfers, please[contact us](/contact-sales) to discuss the Enterprise plan.

Every tier includes a pool of AI usage shared across Grok and Cursor. Within Grok, the pool covers Chat, Imagine, Voice, and Bot. Higher tiers include larger pools, and usage costs vary by product. Your allowance refreshes weekly, so one heavy day never wipes out your whole month.

If you reach your included usage, you can keep going with top-ups, or wait for your allowance to refresh. You’ll always see your remaining usage in settings. See[our docs](https://cursor.com/help/models-and-usage/usage-limits) for more details.

Paid plans include Grok API access. API usage is billed separately at standard model rates and doesn’t draw from your plan’s usage pool. See[model pricing](https://docs.x.ai/developers/pricing).

The self-serve plans include generous usage of Grok models but do not include an allowance for third-party models. Third-party models remain available in Cursor at API list prices. You pay for exactly what you use, and you can set spending caps to manage costs.

All prices are exclusive of any applicable taxes. See our[Subscription Terms](/legal/bundled-subscription-terms) for more information.

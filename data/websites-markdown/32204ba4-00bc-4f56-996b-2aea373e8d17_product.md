## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a Digital Sales Room (DSR)?

![Plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/638e09f4e83fdd19c81b7cc8/638e09f4e83fdd7a301b7cf5_icon_plus.svg)

A shared, branded space where a buyer and seller move through a deal together: resources, next steps, and stakeholders in one link. In Flowla, REX builds and maintains the room for you.

### How is a DSR different from sales enablement or buyer enablement tools?

![Plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/638e09f4e83fdd19c81b7cc8/638e09f4e83fdd7a301b7cf5_icon_plus.svg)

Enablement tools help reps find and share content. A digital sales room is the shared space the buyer actually uses, with engagement tracking and an agent acting on what happens inside it.

### What are common use cases for digital sales rooms?

![Plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/638e09f4e83fdd19c81b7cc8/638e09f4e83fdd7a301b7cf5_icon_plus.svg)

Closing new business, onboarding after close, renewals, and partner deals. The same room carries context across all of them.

### Why should I use a digital sales room?

![Plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/638e09f4e83fdd19c81b7cc8/638e09f4e83fdd7a301b7cf5_icon_plus.svg)

It replaces scattered email threads and attachments with one place the buyer actually returns to, and gives the seller visibility they never get from email alone.

### Can a digital sales room support onboarding and customer success?

![Plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/638e09f4e83fdd19c81b7cc8/638e09f4e83fdd7a301b7cf5_icon_plus.svg)

Yes. The room a deal closes in becomes the onboarding room, so context transfers from sales to CS without a cold handoff.

### How customizable are digital sales rooms?

![Plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/638e09f4e83fdd19c81b7cc8/638e09f4e83fdd7a301b7cf5_icon_plus.svg)

Themes carry your colours, logo, and heading styles into every room automatically, set once at the team level.

### How secure are digital sales rooms?

![Plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/638e09f4e83fdd19c81b7cc8/638e09f4e83fdd7a301b7cf5_icon_plus.svg)

Flowla rooms are SOC 2 certified and GDPR compliant, with permission controls at the room level so you decide what each stakeholder can see.

### What ROI can I expect from a digital sales room?

![Plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/638e09f4e83fdd19c81b7cc8/638e09f4e83fdd7a301b7cf5_icon_plus.svg)

Customers report faster time to implement, higher rep adoption, and more closed won deals. See the results above for figures from mindbody, SLERP, Sourcescrub, and UserGuiding.

### Which tools do digital sales rooms integrate with?

![Plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/638e09f4e83fdd19c81b7cc8/638e09f4e83fdd7a301b7cf5_icon_plus.svg)

Flowla connects with your CRM, call notetaker, email, and collaboration tools, so REX can act on context from every source without you leaving the room.

### What kind of analytics and insights does a DSR provide?

![Plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/638e09f4e83fdd19c81b7cc8/638e09f4e83fdd7a301b7cf5_icon_plus.svg)

You see who viewed what and for how long, and which deals are warming up or going quiet. REX surfaces the signals directly rather than leaving you to read a dashboard.

### Are digital sales rooms suitable for small or growing teams?

![Plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/638e09f4e83fdd19c81b7cc8/638e09f4e83fdd7a301b7cf5_icon_plus.svg)

Yes. Rooms build and maintain themselves, so a small team gets the same consistency as a large one without adding headcount.

### What should I look for when choosing a digital sales room?

![Plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/638e09f4e83fdd19c81b7cc8/638e09f4e83fdd7a301b7cf5_icon_plus.svg)

CRM integration, engagement tracking, mutual action plans, and branding matter. The part most tools miss is an agent that acts on what it sees, rather than just reporting it back to you.

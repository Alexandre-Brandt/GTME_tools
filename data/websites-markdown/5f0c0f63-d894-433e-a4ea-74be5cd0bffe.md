FAQ

## Questions about Alfa.

What is Alfa?

Alfa is an AI-native go-to-market platform that finds your champions (the people inside target accounts most likely to advocate for buying what you sell) and the warm path to reach them. Describe your product in a sentence, and Alfa builds a live stream of matching accounts and champions, drafts the first message, and asks someone who already knows the champion for an introduction when that path exists.

Who is Alfa for?

Anyone who runs outbound: founders doing their own selling, AEs and SDRs, RevOps, and GTM leaders who want their team working one live list instead of rebuilding it every quarter.

What does Alfa include?

Four connected products. Warm intros finds someone on Alfa who knows your champion and asks them to introduce you. Streams turn a description of your buyer into a live, scored pipeline. Focus mode loads the next champion with full context and a pre-written draft. The Leaderboard shows every rep's outbound live.

How is Alfa different from a data provider or a tool like Clay?

Data providers give you records to filter, and toolkits like Clay give you tables and enrichment chains to build. Alfa starts from what you sell, works out which companies and people fit, and keeps that list current as the market moves, so there's nothing to assemble or maintain.

How much does Alfa cost?

Alfa starts with a 14-day free trial, with no credit card and up to 50 champions and 3 streams. Paid plans are Launch at $49/month and Growth at $599/month, both cheaper when billed yearly, and Custom pricing is available on request. See the pricing page for details.

Who uses Alfa?

GTM teams at companies including Vertice, Highnote, GraySwan and MindsHub.

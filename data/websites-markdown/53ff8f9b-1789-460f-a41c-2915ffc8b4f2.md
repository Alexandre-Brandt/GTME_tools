# One platform for every email

Loops is the email sending platform for SaaS teams. Send [marketing](./marketing-email), [product](./product-email), and [transactional](./transactional-email) lifecycle email from one place, from waitlist to IPO.

[Start for free](https://app.loops.so/register)

[Book a demo](https://calendly.com/d/dv7n-xbr-z7w/loops-demo?primary_color=FC5200)

An AI agent sets up a software company’s email lifecycle in Loops through the Loops API: an onboarding workflow triggered when a contact is added, which the user starts; a payment receipt transactional email with its API payload; a product launch campaign scheduled to an Active customers segment; and a win-back workflow left as a draft. Home then lists all four.

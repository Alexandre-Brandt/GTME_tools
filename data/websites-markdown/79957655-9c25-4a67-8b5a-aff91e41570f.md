## Deep research

Twain searches the web in real time at the account and contact level. Get signals from YouTube, Reddit, LinkedIn, and the news. With exact dates and source links.

## Qualification

Twain understands 1st-party and 3rd-party data. Conversation history, deal notes, inbound signals. Then qualifies every lead based on all context before building bespoke outreach strategies.

## Writing

Every message is written from the research and thesis. Not from an LLM trying to fit a pseudo-personalized first line into a template.   
That's how you show effort.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**

After you cancel, you’ll retain access to the Similarweb platform until the end of your current subscription period, and the cancellation will complete automatically when it ends. The cancellation process depends on your Similarweb plan: - Similarweb for Business: Termination terms are defined in your contract. Please contact your account representative or our support team ( [support@similarweb.com](mailto:Support@similarweb.com) ) for assistance with ending your agreement. - Similarweb for Entrepreneurs: If you’re using a Similarweb for Entrepreneurs package, you can cancel directly from your subscription information page within the platform. Once you cancel, your plan will not renew after the current billing period ends.

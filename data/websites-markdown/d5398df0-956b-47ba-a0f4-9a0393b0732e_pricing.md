## What are the best use-cases for emlen?

![Image 768: Arrow down](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/659d62b2d4836a19edbe41d3/65fd7b70f791155e6a04beab_Arrow%20down.svg)

emlen is a sales and buyer enablement solution that makes it easy for sales teams to organize and share sales content, and create a collaborative space for all interactions with B2B buyers. This simplifies the sales process, making it more informed for seller and buyer, and leads to a better overall experience.  
  
emlen use cases are sales content management, sales follow-up, deal prioritization, sales content analytic, and buying center alignment.

## Can I style (whitelabel) digital sales rooms?

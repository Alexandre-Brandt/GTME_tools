## Frequently asked questions

### What's the difference between Pro, Team, and Enterprise?

 +  

Pro is ideal for specific use cases with up to 3 workspaces. Team supports multiple use cases within a department with 20 workspaces and enhanced insights. Enterprise offers unlimited workspaces, and advanced features like regional hosting, log streaming, and on-premise connectivity.

 

### How much does Tray cost?

 +  

Tray starts at $50 a month, and a free trial lets you size your usage.

 

### How does Tray pricing work?

 +  

Tray pricing is based on usage. Integrations, automations, apps and MCP tools are measured in Tasks and Helix Compute Units (HCUs). There are no per-user or per-connection fees, and no runtimes to buy and install, so cost follows what you run, not how many people or systems you connect. Commit to an annual volume for a fixed annual cost, and larger volumes get preferential pricing.

 

### What counts as a Task?

 +  

A Task is Tray's unit of usage: one operation Tray performs on a piece of your data. The trigger firing is one Task, and so is each connector or service step and each pass through a loop or boolean. Think of a Task as one step in a workflow. A Task is not an API call, so a connector step that makes several API calls still counts as one Task. Retries are not charged.

 

### What is an HCU?

 +  

An HCU, or Helix Compute Unit, is the single unit for everything an app uses when it runs: deploy, compute, database, storage, data delivery, and requests. Everything draws from one balance and shows as one number on the bill, so there is no separate charge for hosting, a database or storage.

 

### How predictable is Tray pricing as usage grows?

 +  

You know your annual cost when you sign. Usage dashboards show your Task and HCU usage, and you can set monthly limits. If your usage grows past your plan, the difference is settled at a true-up, and larger volumes get preferential pricing. A free trial shows how much you will use before you buy. Adding users or connections does not change the price.

Tray's free trial is built for exactly that: 30 days and 1 million Tasks on Tray iPaaS, with every capability and the full connector library, and it can include Tray Helix too, with no card and no sales call. Run your real workflows, see exactly how many Tasks they use, and your quote is built on that measured usage rather than an estimate. You get a precise, fixed annual price with no per-user or per-connection fees and no hidden costs. Usage dashboards keep showing your Tasks after you buy, and anything above your plan is settled at a true-up. You can start on the Tray AI Orchestration Platform for $50 a month with Tray Helix Builder Edition.

## Why SaaS companies choose Cello for their partner referral programs

Cello is the easiest way to add a partner referral program to your SaaS product in hours.

##### Tiered Rewards

### Flexible

Cello offers the opportunity to create multiple partner campaigns - whether you want to create tiered reward systems, or just incentivize affiliates with tailored rewards, we've got you covered.

##### Outsource your Work

### Automated

Cello allows you to automate referral payouts, handling all KYC, compliance, and fraud detection. Simplify sharing and remove the stress.

##### Free to start

### No upfront costs

Unlike most partner referral tools, Cello is free to get started. You only pay as your referral ARR grows - so the channel proves itself before you spend anything.

## How Cello scales your product

Integrate user and partner referrals for maximum impact and growth.

### Easy setup, multiple campaigns

Integrate partner referrals with Cello effortlessly, design multiple reward campaigns, and efficiently manage partners from your client portal to enhance engagement and referral growth. And you can get started for free.

![]()

### Partner portal

Provide partners with a dedicated portal, enabling easy access to resources, referral links and immediate rewards; offer comprehensive analytics to monitor and optimize referral performance.

![]()

### Track your partners' performance

Access detailed reports and analytics to evaluate your partner program's performance, monitor individual affiliate performance, and experiment with different strategies to maximize impact and efficiency.

![]()

### Onboard partners in minutes, not days

Add partners individually, then assign them to the right campaign instantly. Run different reward structures for different partner types - affiliates, influencers, resellers - all from one place. No manual setup, no back-and-forth.

![]()

### Automate your work

Delegate KYC and compliance tasks to Cello, implement automated referral payments, and free up time for strategic initiatives and core business activities.

![]()

### Give partners everything they need to sell

Upload guides, videos, and program details directly to your partner portal. Partners get instant access to resources, referral links, and reward info - so they can hit the ground running without chasing you for materials.

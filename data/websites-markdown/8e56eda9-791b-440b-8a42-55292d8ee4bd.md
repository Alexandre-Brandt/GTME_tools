The companies of the future will run on a world model of their business, not a Salesforce-era database.

For agents to do customer-facing work, they need to understand how your business actually works. Legacy CRMs weren’t built for that. Layer agents on top and you get low-quality work, because the underlying data lacks the structure agents need to reason from.

Lightfield reimagines the CRM as a world model of your business. It updates itself from every call, email, meeting, and Slack thread, assembling a context graph for agents to work from.

Our agents find prospects that look like your best customers and book meetings with them. They capture commitments from every conversation and handle the follow-ups, diagnose where your funnel is weak, and learn from your best sellers to codify what works.

And your whole company works from a shared understanding of your customers.

## Instructions — SKILL.md

### Overview

Read the deal, not the fields. Pull the last three interactions from the timeline and judge against what was actually said on calls and in email, not against what someone typed into a stage picker.

Return one next step, never a list. Name the specific evidence that’s missing and who would have it.

Cite the section of the framework behind every judgment, so a new rep learns the process instead of just receiving an answer.

## How Lightfield works

Platform

2.0

* Full capture

  ### A record that updates itself

  Connect email, calendar, Slack, LinkedIn, and your other systems in minutes. Lightfield captures every customer interaction and keeps the record structured automatically.
* System of record

  ### A data model designed for agents

  Custom objects and relationships mirror how your business works. A temporal context graph connects every interaction and preserves how your data changes over time.
* Agent infrastructure

  ### An agent harness for reliable work

  A standardized SDK, deterministic code sandbox, and continuous evals give agents consistent access to records and dependable outputs across every run.
* Open platform

  ### A flexible platform to build on

  Read and write every record through API, MCP, and CLI. Build agents, automations, and workflows on shared data, then connect them to the tools you already use.

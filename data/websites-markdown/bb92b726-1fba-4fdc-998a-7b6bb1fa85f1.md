### What is Leadsforge, and how does it compare to other tools?

Leadsforge is a lead search engine you talk to. Instead of building queries out of dropdown filters, you describe the company and the person you want, and it returns a matched, verified list drawn from multiple data sources. Behind the chat, waterfall enrichment keeps pulling from source to source until the right email, LinkedIn profile or phone number is found. Most prospecting tools ask you to learn their filter logic first. This one asks you to describe your buyer.

### What Data Sources Does Leadsforge Use & How Accurate Is The Data?

Every lookup runs through a waterfall across multiple verified B2B data providers, covering more than 500M contacts globally. If the first source cannot return verified data, the next is queried automatically until the best available result comes back. Emails are validated in real time, LinkedIn URLs are confirmed against live profiles, and phone numbers pass format and validity checks, which is why lists come out cleaner than single-source tools manage.

### Does Leadsforge Integrate with Other Tools?

Yes. Lists export to CSV in one click, or move directly into Salesforge without an export step. Nothing Leadsforge finds is locked in.

### Can I Push Lists Directly Into Multi-Channel Sequences (email + LinkedIn) in Salesforge?

Absolutely. A list generated or enriched in Leadsforge pushes straight into Salesforge with no copy-paste or file import, and from there you can launch email and LinkedIn sequences that adapt to how prospects respond. The gap between building a list and sending to it is minutes.

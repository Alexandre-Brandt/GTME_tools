[### Email Finder](/email-finder)

A website, a first name, and a last name are all it takes to get a 100% GDPR-compliant, nominative, and verified email address.   
  
Dropcontact does not rely on third-party email verification services. Every email is verified in real time using proprietary technology developed and operated in-house, delivering exceptional validity rates of 99% for nominative emails and 97% for catch-all emails.  
  
And Dropcontact goes even further: automatic enrichment, cleaning, duplicate detection, and merging.  
  
[Discover our Email Finder](/email-finder)

[### CRM enrichment](/crm-features)

Dropcontact is the all-in-one solution that natively integrates with Pipedrive, HubSpot, and soon Salesforce and Zoho CRM.   
  
The plug-and-play setup takes less than 5 minutes and only requires CRM admin rights. Your data is enriched directly inside your CRM.   
  
No more time wasted managing data quality, your teams can finally focus on what really matters 💚  
  
Discover CRM enrichment in [HubSpot](/hubspot) and [Pipedrive](/pipedrive)

[### Company enrichment](/main-features)

Dropcontact enriches the following company data: website, LinkedIn page, industry sector, NAF code, salary range, SIREN number, and intra-community VAT number.

[### GDPR](/main-features)

**The only 100% GDPR compliant solution.**Most enrichment tools on the market rely on personal databases.   
  
But every individual in those databases must have given explicit consent for their data to be used by third parties. Are you sure you have that consent?  
  
Unlike other tools, Dropcontact uses proprietary algorithms and test servers to find, verify, and validate all data. No personal information is stored or resold.

[### Company change detection](/main-features)

When uploading your database file, Dropcontact will detect if one of your contacts has changed company and will enrich each of these contacts with new updated data! 💚  
  
Dropcontact recognizes your contact's new company and enriches their record with the following data:  
‍  
- New nominative and verified email address  
- New professionnal phone number  
- New job   
- New website and new LinkedIn company page  
- New company legal information  
  
[Discover company change detection](/company-change-alert)

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do you offer annual billing?

All plans are billed monthly. Contact us if you need flexible invoicing terms.

Does Teamfluence integrate with my CRM?

Yes. HubSpot and Salesforce connect natively. Other tools connect through webhooks, our API or tools like Zapier and Make.

How much does Teamfluence cost?

One plan for everything: €449 per month. It includes unlimited seats, 1,000 qualified leads per month and 20,000 credits.

I'm moving from Trigify. Can you help?

Yes. We price-match and migrate your setup for free. Talk to us.

Is there a free trial?

No. We don't do self-service sign-ups. Every customer starts with a call and a guided onboarding, so your workspace is set up properly from day one.

Is there a limit on networking campaigns?

No. You can run as many campaigns as you like. Daily limits per person apply.

What are credits?

Credits cover the work done on your leads, like enrichment, AI qualification and workflows. The plan includes more than enough for normal use. They're there to keep usage fair, not to charge you extra.

What counts as a qualified lead?

A lead counts the first time you move it out of Teamfluence: you push it to your CRM, export it to CSV, send it by webhook, get it in Slack or pull it through the API. Moving the same person again doesn't count twice. Everyone else Teamfluence finds stays in your workspace and doesn't count, and neither does the 90 days of history we import when you start.

What does "pay for leads, not for seats" mean?

You can add your whole team at no extra cost. The price depends on how many qualified leads you move into your CRM or other tools each month, not on how many people use Teamfluence.

What happens if I need more than 1,000 leads a month?

You can add another bucket of leads and credits. Your plan grows with your results.

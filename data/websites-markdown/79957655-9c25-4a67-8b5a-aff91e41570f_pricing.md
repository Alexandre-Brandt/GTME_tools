## Three research depths

Every lead is researched before Twain writes anything. With modes, you choose how much research depth goes into each prospect.

## Base

1 credit per lead. A single search on the account and the contact, then a sequence written strictly to your instructions. Built for volume.

## High

2 credits per lead. Multi-search. Adds LinkedIn and YouTube activity, and a thesis on whether the lead is worth reaching out to and why now.

## Ultra

Starts at 10 credits per lead.  
Runs most searches. Finds missing data. Reasons through an agentic thesis and strategy. Writes the most human messages. Requires no instructions.

Plans and credits Using Twain

*   How much does Twain cost?How much does Twain cost?  

We give new accounts 500 free credits for 30 days, with no credit card required. Prices are in USD. Taxes are added where applicable.

Monthly subscriptions add credits each month:

    *   Starter: 500 credits for $49 per month.
    *   Starter: 1,000 credits for $99 per month.
    *   Pro: 2,000 credits for $198 per month.
    *   Pro: 4,000 credits for $396 per month.
    *   Pro: 10,000 credits for $900 per month.
    *   Pro: 14,000 credits for $1,260 per month.
    *   For Enterprise plans with custom credit volumes, contact sales for pricing.

One-time credit packs (subscription required):

    *   200 credits for $30.
    *   500 credits for $74.
    *   1,000 credits for $149.
    *   1,500 credits for $203.
    *   2,000 credits for $270.
    *   4,000 credits for $480.
    *   10,000 credits for $1,150.

*   Do subscription credits roll over?Do subscription credits roll over?  Unused credits roll over on Pro and Enterprise plans while your subscription is active. At renewal, we cap the balance at three times the monthly credits in your plan. 
*   When do one-time credits expire?When do one-time credits expire?  One-time credits expire after 60 days. If you buy another pack, we extend your entire remaining balance to 60 days from that purchase. 
*   What happens to my credits if I cancel?What happens to my credits if I cancel?  You can use your remaining credits until five days after your paid period ends. 
*   Can I try Twain for free?Can I try Twain for free?  Yes. New accounts receive 500 free credits for 30 days, and you do not need a credit card.

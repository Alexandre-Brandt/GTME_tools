## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long are these contracts?**   
 Every plan is paid for a set period: a month or a year, and Pro also for a day or a week. Auto-renewal is optional - you can choose it when paying for a month or a year - and can be cancelled any time.

**Is there a free trial or demo?**   
 There's no free trial, but you are able to test out what PublicWWW has to offer for free. That way you can be sure before you buy.

## **Call Center Features**

![Real-Time Modifications](https://a.storyblok.com/f/157376/x/fc91d62164/edit.svg)

### Real-Time Modifications

Toggle your system settings at any time, including adding new numbers or users in a single click.

![Call Queuing](https://a.storyblok.com/f/157376/x/3677c5e17d/queue.svg)

### Call Queuing

Give inbound callers the option to remain in queue until one of your agents is available to talk.

![Ring Groups](https://a.storyblok.com/f/157376/x/c31f328872/team.svg)

### Ring Groups

Route calls to teams grouped by location, language, skill, or any other trait you’d like.

![Parallel Calls](https://a.storyblok.com/f/157376/x/015750430d/parallel.svg)

![Unlimited Concurrent Calls](https://a.storyblok.com/f/157376/x/c7eca4dc66/pad.svg)

### Unlimited Concurrent Calls

Take or place as many calls as you would like — simultaneously — on the same phone number.

![Queue Callback](https://a.storyblok.com/f/157376/x/4219a2c526/callback.svg)

### Queue Callback

Give callers the power to opt out of a waiting line and receive your outbound reply at a later time.

![Time and date-based routing](https://a.storyblok.com/f/157376/x/1828f67898/calendar.svg)

### Time and date-based routing

Provide continuous service on public holidays, and during and after business hours. Customize call flows based on holidays, peak hours, and downtime to reduce your percentage of missed calls.

### Call Recording

Review call recordings to help confirm details, monitor quality, and guide training sessions.

![Pause-Resume Recording](https://a.storyblok.com/f/157376/x/d5707e728e/pause_record.svg)

### Pause-Resume Recording

Keep your callers’ sensitive information secure by temporarily pausing call recording as needed.

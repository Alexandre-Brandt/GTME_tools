## Your questions, answered

How do I identify website visitors on my site?

Leadfeeder identifies the companies visiting your website in real time, including those who never fill out a form. By identifying website visitors at the company level, you can see [which organisations are showing interest]( /solutions/buying-intent-signals/), what pages they browse, and when they’re most active. It takes less than five minutes to set up.

Does Leadfeeder work as a B2B website visitor tracking tool?

Yes. Leadfeeder is B2B website visitor tracking software that tracks the companies visiting your website in real time, identifying who they are, which pages they browse, and how often they return. Filter by firmographic criteria to focus on your ICP, set alerts when target accounts visit, and push everything to your [CRM.](/integrations/) All tracking is at the company level and processed in line with [GDPR.](/gdpr/)

What is the best website visitor identification software?

The best website visitor identification software combines high match rates, real-time company-level identification, GDPR-compliant EU data, and [native CRM integrations](/integrations/). Leadfeeder identifies your B2B visitors at company level and pushes high-fit accounts straight to your CRM, with a [free plan to start.](/pricing/)

Can I see who visits my website?

Yes, at the company level. Leadfeeder shows you which companies visit your website, which pages they view, and how often they return. Individual visitors are not shown by default. This is what makes the approach GDPR-compliant: we identify the company behind a visit, not the individual. You’ll only see a person’s contact details if they identify themselves, for example by submitting a form.

How does website visitor tracking work?

You add a single tracking script to your website. Leadfeeder then tracks each visit and matches the associated IP address against a continuously updated company database. Bots, internet service providers, and low-value traffic are automatically filtered out. What remains is a list of real companies and the pages they visited. Tracking works whether or not a visitor fills out a form.

How can I identify website visitors?

You can identify website visitors using a B2B visitor identification solution. Google Analytics only tells you how many people visited your website. Leadfeeder shows you which companies they came from. Once the tracking script is installed, you’ll start seeing identified companies along with details such as their industry, company size, location, and the pages they visited.

How much does website visitor identification cost?

Leadfeeder offers a free plan as well as paid plans based on the number of companies identified each month. You can try it free for 14 days with no credit card required. You’ll find all the details on our [pricing page.](/pricing/)

Can Leadfeeder identify companies whose employees work remotely?

Yes, unlike some of our competitors, Leadfeeder can identify the companies even if their employees work remotely.
When people work remotely, they usually use dynamic IP addresses that change ownership frequently, making them more difficult to track.
Our IP database receives a constant stream of updates from multiple data sources to stay up to date when ownership of IP addresses changes.

How is Leadfeeder data kept accurate and up to date?

Leadfeeder automatically removes bots, ISPs, and other low-quality traffic sources, so you only get a list of high-quality identified companies.
Unlike other tools, you also have the option to remove unwanted companies yourself. This makes Leadfeeder data quality superior to others.
Our unique machine learning algorithm creates a fresh and accurate version of the IP-to-company database every day. It uses our proprietary data and combines it with millions of external signals and public IP registration databases. Every month around 20% of the database changes, so updating it on a daily basis is important.

Is Leadfeeder GDPR compliant?

Leadfeeder is a fully [GDPR-compliant solution.](/gdpr/) It collects behavioral data of the companies visiting your website, including the pages viewed, where they came from, and how long they spent on your site.
IP addresses are collected to determine the company and its geographic location. By default Leadfeeder only shows company visits, not individuals. Leadfeeder only tracks individuals when you opt in and when the individuals identify themselves on your website (via form submissions or email integrations).
Leadfeeder also enriches company visit data with publicly available business contact data.
